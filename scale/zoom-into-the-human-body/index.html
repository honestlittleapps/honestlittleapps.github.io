What you just scrolled through

From your full height to a single atom is a zoom of about 10 billion times. Every level in between is busy: cells dividing, blood cells being made at about 2 million a second, and DNA two metres long folded into each cell.

Questions

How many cells are in the human body?

About 30 trillion human cells in an average adult, according to a widely cited 2016 estimate. Most of them, about 25 trillion, are red blood cells.

How big is a red blood cell?

About 7 to 8 micrometres across, roughly a tenth the width of a hair.

How long is the DNA in one cell?

About 2 metres if stretched out, packed into a nucleus a few micrometres wide.
