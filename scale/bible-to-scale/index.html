What the shape tells you

More than three quarters of the chapters (929 of 1,189) are in the Old Testament. Psalms alone has 150, more than the whole of Paul's letters (87). The four Gospels together have 89 chapters, so a chapter a day takes you through the life of Jesus in about three months.

Questions

How many chapters are in the Bible?

1,189 in the 66-book Protestant Bible: 929 in the Old Testament and 260 in the New Testament.

What is the longest book of the Bible?

Psalms, with 150 chapters. The shortest books by chapter count have just one chapter: Obadiah, Philemon, 2 John, 3 John and Jude.

How long does it take to read the whole Bible?

At about 3¼ chapters a day you finish in a year (1,189 ÷ 365). Use the slider on this page to see your own finish date.
