Questions

How tall is an average thunderstorm?

Ordinary summer thunderstorms often reach 30,000 to 50,000 feet. The strongest supercells can overshoot to 60,000 feet or more.

Why is the top of a thunderstorm flat?

The rising air reaches the tropopause, where temperature stops falling with height. The air can't easily keep rising, so it spreads out into a flat anvil.

How far away can lightning strike?

Up to about 10 miles from the storm. That's why the National Weather Service says to go indoors as soon as you hear thunder and wait 30 minutes after the last rumble.
