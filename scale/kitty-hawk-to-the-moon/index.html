What you just scrolled through

The first powered flight lasted 12 seconds and was shorter than a modern airliner's wingspan. One human lifetime later, people were walking on the Moon, carrying a piece of that first airplane with them.

Questions

How long was the Wright brothers' first flight?

12 seconds and 120 feet, flown by Orville Wright on December 17, 1903. The longest flight that day, by Wilbur, lasted 59 seconds and covered 852 feet.

How many years between the first flight and the Moon landing?

65 years and 7 months: December 17, 1903 to July 20, 1969.

Did Neil Armstrong take a piece of the Wright Flyer to the Moon?

Yes. He carried a piece of muslin fabric from the Flyer's wing and a piece of wood from its propeller on Apollo 11.
