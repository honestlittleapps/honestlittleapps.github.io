Infused Water Recipes

Plain water is easy to forget. A jug of fruit water in the fridge isn't. Each of these takes five minutes and makes about two litres.

Cucumber Mint Water

Cool, crisp and spa-like. The easiest infused water there is.

Prep 5 minTotal 1 hr 5 minMakes 1 pitcher (about 8 cups)

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cucumber, thinly sliced
  • 8 fresh mint leaves
  • 8 cups cold water
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Slice the cucumber thinly.
  2. Clap the mint leaves between your hands once to release the flavour.
  3. Add cucumber and mint to a pitcher, then pour in the cold water.
  4. Chill for at least 1 hour, then serve over ice.

Strawberry Basil Water

Sweet strawberry with a soft herby finish.

Prep 5 minTotal 1 hr 5 minMakes 1 pitcher (about 8 cups)

Ingredients

  • 6 strawberries, hulled and sliced
  • 6 fresh basil leaves
  • 8 cups cold water
  • Ice

Steps

  1. Slice the strawberries.
  2. Tear the basil leaves in half.
  3. Add both to a pitcher and pour in the water.
  4. Chill for 1 to 2 hours, then serve over ice.

Lemon Ginger Water

Bright lemon with a gentle ginger warmth.

Prep 5 minTotal 2 hr 5 minMakes 1 pitcher (about 8 cups)

Ingredients

  • 1 lemon, sliced into rounds
  • 1-inch piece fresh ginger, thinly sliced
  • 8 cups cold water

Steps

  1. Slice the lemon and ginger as thinly as you can.
  2. Add them to a pitcher with the water.
  3. Chill for 2 hours so the ginger can infuse.
  4. Remove the lemon after about 4 hours if you don't like bitterness.

Orange Cinnamon Water

A cozy winter flavour that tastes like the holidays.

Prep 5 minTotal 2 hr 5 minMakes 1 pitcher (about 8 cups)

Ingredients

  • 1 orange, sliced into rounds
  • 2 cinnamon sticks
  • 8 cups cold water

Steps

  1. Slice the orange.
  2. Add the orange and cinnamon sticks to a pitcher.
  3. Pour in the water and chill for 2 hours.
  4. Serve cold, or warm a mug of it gently on cold days.

Cranberry Rosemary Holiday Water

Festive enough for the holiday table, no sugar needed.

Prep 5 minTotal 1 hr 5 minMakes 1 pitcher (about 8 cups)

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup fresh cranberries, halved
  • 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
  • 1 orange slice
  • 8 cups cold water or sparkling water

Steps

  1. Halve the cranberries so they release colour.
  2. Add cranberries, rosemary and the orange slice to a pitcher.
  3. Pour in the water. Use sparkling water just before serving for a party version.
  4. Chill for 1 hour and serve.

Apple Cinnamon Water

Like apple pie in a glass, without the sugar.

Prep 5 minTotal 2 hr 5 minMakes 1 pitcher (about 8 cups)

Ingredients

  • 1 apple, thinly sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 8 cups cold water

Steps

  1. Thinly slice the apple and remove the seeds.
  2. Add apple slices and the cinnamon stick to a pitcher.
  3. Pour in the water.
  4. Chill for 2 hours before serving.

Questions

How long does infused water last?

Keep it in the fridge and drink it within about a day for the best taste. Remove citrus after a few hours if it starts to taste bitter.

Can I refill the pitcher with the same fruit?

Yes, once. The second batch will taste lighter.

Does infused water count toward hydration?

Yes. It's water with a little flavour, so it counts like plain water.
