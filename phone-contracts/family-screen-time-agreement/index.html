Questions

Should parents follow screen-time rules too?

Rules that apply to everyone are easier for kids to accept and model the habit you want them to learn.

What are good family screen-time rules?

Common ones are no phones at meals, a device bedtime with charging outside bedrooms, and one screen-free block each weekend.

Is my information saved?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser until you print.
