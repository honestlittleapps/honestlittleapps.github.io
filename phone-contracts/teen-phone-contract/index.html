Questions

Do teens need a phone contract?

Not every family uses one, but writing rules down together helps avoid arguments, because everyone agreed to the same thing ahead of time.

How strict should a teen phone contract be?

Most families start with clear night-time and driving rules, then loosen others as trust grows. Building in a regular review helps.

Is my information saved?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser until you print.
