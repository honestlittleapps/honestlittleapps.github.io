Questions

What age should a child get a first phone?

There's no single right age. Many families start around 10 to 13, often with a basic phone or strict limits first. Maturity matters more than the number.

What should a first phone contract include?

Bedtime charging, school rules, app permissions, online safety, kindness, screen-time limits and clear consequences. Including promises from the parent makes it feel fair.

Is my information saved?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser until you print.
