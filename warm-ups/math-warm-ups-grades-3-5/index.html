Math Warm-Ups for Grades 3–5

Three quick questions to start math class: one multiplication fact, one word problem, and one rounding, fractions, place value or perimeter question.

It opens on a different day each school day, so you can bookmark it and put it on the board every morning. Pick any day from the menu, or link straight to one with ?day=5.

    How to use it

    • Put it on the projector as students come in. Start the 60-second timer and let them work on whiteboards or in notebooks.
    • Press Show answers to go over them together. The whole routine takes about five minutes.
    • Need a printed copy or a sub plan? The full list with answers is below.
