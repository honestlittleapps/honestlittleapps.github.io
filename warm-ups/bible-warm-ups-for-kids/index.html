Bible Warm-Ups for Sunday School and Christian Classrooms

Three quick questions to open class: fill in the missing word of a King James verse, name the book it comes from, and one Bible fact.

It opens on a different day each school day, so you can bookmark it and put it on the board every morning. Pick any day from the menu, or link straight to one with ?day=5.

    How to use it

    • Put it on the projector as students come in. Start the 60-second timer and let them work on whiteboards or in notebooks.
    • Press Show answers to go over them together. The whole routine takes about five minutes.
    • Need a printed copy or a sub plan? The full list with answers is below.

    All 20 days with answers (printable)

    Day 1

    1. Fill in the blank: “In the beginning God _____ the heaven and the earth.”
      Answer: created
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Genesis 1:1
    3. How many books are in the Bible?
      Answer: 66

    Day 2

    1. Fill in the blank: “For God so _____ the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”
      Answer: loved
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: John 3:16
    3. What is the first book of the New Testament?
      Answer: Matthew

    Day 3

    1. Fill in the blank: “The LORD is my _____; I shall not want.”
      Answer: shepherd
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Psalm 23:1
    3. What is the last book of the Old Testament?
      Answer: Malachi

    Day 4

    1. Fill in the blank: “Thy word is a _____ unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”
      Answer: lamp
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Psalm 119:105
    3. How many disciples did Jesus choose?
      Answer: Twelve

    Day 5

    1. Fill in the blank: “_____ in the LORD with all thine heart; and lean not unto thine own understanding.”
      Answer: Trust
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Proverbs 3:5
    3. Who built the ark?
      Answer: Noah (Genesis 6)

    Day 6

    1. Fill in the blank: “I can do all things through _____ which strengtheneth me.”
      Answer: Christ
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Philippians 4:13
    3. Who was swallowed by a great fish?
      Answer: Jonah (Jonah 1:17)

    Day 7

    1. Fill in the blank: “Be strong and of a good _____; be not afraid, neither be thou dismayed: for the LORD thy God is with thee whithersoever thou goest.”
      Answer: courage
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Joshua 1:9
    3. In which town was Jesus born?
      Answer: Bethlehem

    Day 8

    1. Fill in the blank: “Ye are the _____ of the world. A city that is set on an hill cannot be hid.”
      Answer: light
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Matthew 5:14
    3. What did God make on the first day?
      Answer: Light (Genesis 1:3)

    Day 9

    1. Fill in the blank: “We love him, because he _____ loved us.”
      Answer: first
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: 1 John 4:19
    3. Who defeated Goliath?
      Answer: David (1 Samuel 17)

    Day 10

    1. Fill in the blank: “This is the day which the LORD hath made; we will _____ and be glad in it.”
      Answer: rejoice
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Psalm 118:24
    3. How many days and nights did it rain in the flood?
      Answer: Forty (Genesis 7:12)

    Day 11

    1. Fill in the blank: “Children, _____ your parents in the Lord: for this is right.”
      Answer: obey
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Ephesians 6:1
    3. Who was thrown into the lions' den?
      Answer: Daniel (Daniel 6)

    Day 12

    1. Fill in the blank: “Be _____, and know that I am God…”
      Answer: still
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Psalm 46:10
    3. What is the shortest verse in the Bible?
      Answer: “Jesus wept.” (John 11:35)

    Day 13

    1. Fill in the blank: “Jesus Christ the same _____, and to day, and for ever.”
      Answer: yesterday
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Hebrews 13:8
    3. Who led Israel out of Egypt?
      Answer: Moses

    Day 14

    1. Fill in the blank: “_____ without ceasing.”
      Answer: Pray
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: 1 Thessalonians 5:17
    3. Which book has the most chapters?
      Answer: Psalms (150)

    Day 15

    1. Fill in the blank: “A _____ loveth at all times, and a brother is born for adversity.”
      Answer: friend
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Proverbs 17:17
    3. Who was the first king of Israel?
      Answer: Saul

    Day 16

    1. Fill in the blank: “For unto you is born this day in the city of David a _____, which is Christ the Lord.”
      Answer: Saviour
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Luke 2:11
    3. How many books are in the New Testament?
      Answer: 27

    Day 17

    1. Fill in the blank: “I will praise thee; for I am fearfully and _____ made…”
      Answer: wonderfully
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Psalm 139:14
    3. In which river was Jesus baptised?
      Answer: The Jordan

    Day 18

    1. Fill in the blank: “Thou shalt love thy _____ as thyself.”
      Answer: neighbour
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Matthew 22:39
    3. Who denied Jesus three times?
      Answer: Peter

    Day 19

    1. Fill in the blank: “But they that wait upon the LORD shall renew their strength; they shall mount up with wings as _____…”
      Answer: eagles
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Isaiah 40:31
    3. In which book are the Ten Commandments first given?
      Answer: Exodus (chapter 20)

    Day 20

    1. Fill in the blank: “Be not overcome of evil, but overcome evil with _____.”
      Answer: good
    2. Which book is that verse from?
      Answer: Romans 12:21
    3. Who was Abraham's wife?
      Answer: Sarah
