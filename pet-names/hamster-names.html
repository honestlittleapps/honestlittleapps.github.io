How to choose a name

  • Say it out loud. One or two syllables are easiest to call, especially for hamsters.
  • Watch their personality for a few days. A sleepy one might be “Mochi”, a zoomy one “Turbo”.
  • Avoid names that sound like commands you use a lot, like “No” or “Sit”.
