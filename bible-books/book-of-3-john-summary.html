3 John: Summary & Key Themes

3 John at a glance, before you read it.

Written byJohn, “the elder”
Written toGaius
TypeLetter
Chapters1
PositionBook 64 of 66 · book 25 of the New Testament

Summary

John commends Gaius for his hospitality to traveling teachers and rebukes Diotrephes for seeking to be first.

Main themes

  • hospitality
  • truth
  • good example

Key verse

I have no greater joy than to hear that my children walk in truth.
3 John 1:4 (KJV)

How to read 3 John

Read it in one or two sittings first; short letters were written to be read aloud in one go. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
