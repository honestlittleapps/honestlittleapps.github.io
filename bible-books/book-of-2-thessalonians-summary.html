2 Thessalonians: Summary & Key Themes

2 Thessalonians at a glance, before you read it.

Written byPaul
Written tothe church in Thessalonica
TypeLetter
Chapters3
PositionBook 53 of 66 · book 14 of the New Testament

Summary

Paul corrects confusion about the day of the Lord, encourages believers under persecution and tells idle people to work.

Main themes

  • the day of the Lord
  • perseverance
  • work

Key verse

But ye, brethren, be not weary in well doing.
2 Thessalonians 3:13 (KJV)

How to read 2 Thessalonians

Read it in one or two sittings first; short letters were written to be read aloud in one go. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
