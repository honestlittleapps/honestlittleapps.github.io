2 Samuel: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of 2 Samuel at a glance, before you read it.

Written byunknown
Written forIsrael
TypeHistory
Chapters24
PositionBook 10 of 66 · book 10 of the Old Testament

Summary

David reigns over Judah and then all Israel, makes Jerusalem his capital and receives God’s promise of a lasting throne. His sin with Bathsheba brings painful consequences in his family.

Main themes

  • God’s covenant with David
  • sin and repentance
  • consequences

Key verse

And thine house and thy kingdom shall be established for ever before thee: thy throne shall be established for ever.
2 Samuel 7:16 (KJV)

How to read 2 Samuel

Try one chapter a day: 24 days to finish. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
