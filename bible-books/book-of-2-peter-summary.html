2 Peter: Summary & Key Themes

2 Peter at a glance, before you read it.

Chapters3
PositionBook 61 of 66 · book 22 of the New Testament

Summary

Peter warns against false teachers, urges growth in godliness and explains why the Lord’s return has not yet come: He is patient, not willing that any should perish.

Main themes

  • spiritual growth
  • false teaching
  • Christ’s return

Key verse

The Lord is not slack concerning his promise, as some men count slackness; but is longsuffering to us-ward, not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance.
2 Peter 3:9 (KJV)

How to read 2 Peter

Read it in one or two sittings first; short letters were written to be read aloud in one go. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
