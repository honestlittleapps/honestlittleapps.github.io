1 Kings: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of 1 Kings at a glance, before you read it.

Written byunknown
Written forIsrael in exile
TypeHistory
Chapters22
PositionBook 11 of 66 · book 11 of the Old Testament

Summary

Solomon builds the temple but turns to other gods; the kingdom splits in two. Elijah confronts Ahab and the prophets of Baal on Mount Carmel.

Main themes

  • wisdom and its loss
  • a divided kingdom
  • true and false worship

Key verse

And Elijah came unto all the people, and said, How long halt ye between two opinions? if the LORD be God, follow him: but if Baal, then follow him. And the people answered him not a word.
1 Kings 18:21 (KJV)
