Obadiah: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of Obadiah at a glance, before you read it.

Written byObadiah
Written forJudah, about Edom
TypeMinor prophet
Chapters1
PositionBook 31 of 66 · book 31 of the Old Testament

Summary

The shortest Old Testament book announces judgment on Edom for its pride and its cruelty to Judah.

Main themes

  • pride
  • justice
  • God’s kingdom

Key verse

For the day of the LORD is near upon all the heathen: as thou hast done, it shall be done unto thee: thy reward shall return upon thine own head.
Obadiah 1:15 (KJV)

How to read Obadiah

Read it in one sitting first; it is short enough. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
