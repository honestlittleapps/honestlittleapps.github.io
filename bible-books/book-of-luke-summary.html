Luke: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of Luke at a glance, before you read it.

Written byLuke, the physician and companion of Paul (traditional)
Written toTheophilus and a wider Gentile audience
TypeGospel
Chapters24
PositionBook 42 of 66 · book 3 of the New Testament

Summary

Luke writes an orderly account of Jesus’ life, with the fullest story of His birth and parables found nowhere else, such as the good Samaritan and the prodigal son. It highlights Jesus’ care for the poor, women, outsiders and sinners.

Main themes

  • salvation for all people
  • Jesus’ compassion
  • prayer and the Holy Spirit

Key verse

For the Son of man is come to seek and to save that which was lost.
Luke 19:10 (KJV)

How to read Luke

Try one chapter a day: 24 days to finish. Read the whole book quickly once, then slowly. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
