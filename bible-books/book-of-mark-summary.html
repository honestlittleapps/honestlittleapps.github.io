Mark: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of Mark at a glance, before you read it.

Written byJohn Mark, companion of Peter (traditional)
Written toGentile, likely Roman, believers
TypeGospel
Chapters16
PositionBook 41 of 66 · book 2 of the New Testament

Summary

Mark is the shortest Gospel and moves quickly, often using the word “straightway.” It shows Jesus as the servant who acts with authority, and about a third of the book covers His final week, death and resurrection.

Main themes

  • Jesus the servant
  • discipleship and the cross
  • Jesus’ authority

Key verse

For even the Son of man came not to be ministered unto, but to minister, and to give his life a ransom for many.
Mark 10:45 (KJV)

How to read Mark

Try one chapter a day: 16 days to finish. Read the whole book quickly once, then slowly. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
