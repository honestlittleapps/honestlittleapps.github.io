Titus: Summary & Key Themes

Titus at a glance, before you read it.

Written byPaul
Written toTitus, serving in Crete
TypeLetter
Chapters3
PositionBook 56 of 66 · book 17 of the New Testament

Summary

Paul tells Titus to appoint elders and teach believers to live self-controlled, godly lives that match the grace of God.

Main themes

  • good works
  • sound teaching
  • grace that trains

Key verse

For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men,
Titus 2:11 (KJV)

How to read Titus

Read it in one or two sittings first; short letters were written to be read aloud in one go. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
