Daniel: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of Daniel at a glance, before you read it.

Written byDaniel (traditional)
Written forexiles
TypeMajor prophet
Chapters12
PositionBook 27 of 66 · book 27 of the Old Testament

Summary

Daniel and his friends stay faithful in Babylon: the fiery furnace, the writing on the wall and the lions’ den, followed by visions of kingdoms and God’s eternal kingdom.

Main themes

  • faithfulness under pressure
  • God rules over kings
  • hope

Key verse

My God hath sent his angel, and hath shut the lions’ mouths, that they have not hurt me: forasmuch as before him innocency was found in me; and also before thee, O king, have I done no hurt.
Daniel 6:22 (KJV)

How to read Daniel

Try one chapter a day: 12 days to finish. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
