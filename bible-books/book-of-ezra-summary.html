Ezra: Summary & Key Themes

The Book of Ezra at a glance, before you read it.

Written byEzra (traditional)
Written forreturned exiles
TypeHistory
Chapters10
PositionBook 15 of 66 · book 15 of the Old Testament

Summary

Exiles return from Babylon to rebuild the temple, and later Ezra teaches God’s law and calls the people back to faithfulness.

Main themes

  • return and rebuilding
  • Scripture
  • repentance

Key verse

For Ezra had prepared his heart to seek the law of the LORD , and to do it , and to teach in Israel statutes and judgments.
Ezra 7:10 (KJV)

How to read Ezra

Try one chapter a day: 10 days to finish. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
