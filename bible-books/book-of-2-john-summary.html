2 John: Summary & Key Themes

2 John at a glance, before you read it.

Written byJohn, “the elder”
Written tothe “elect lady” and her children
TypeLetter
Chapters1
PositionBook 63 of 66 · book 24 of the New Testament

Summary

A brief letter urging believers to walk in truth and love and not to welcome false teachers.

Main themes

  • truth
  • love
  • discernment

Key verse

And this is love, that we walk after his commandments. This is the commandment, That, as ye have heard from the beginning, ye should walk in it.
2 John 1:6 (KJV)

How to read 2 John

Read it in one or two sittings first; short letters were written to be read aloud in one go. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
