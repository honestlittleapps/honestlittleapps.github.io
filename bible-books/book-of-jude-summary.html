Jude: Summary & Key Themes

Jude at a glance, before you read it.

Written byJude, brother of James (traditional)
Written tobelievers
TypeLetter
Chapters1
PositionBook 65 of 66 · book 26 of the New Testament

Summary

Jude urges believers to contend for the faith against ungodly teachers and closes with a famous blessing to Him who is able to keep you from falling.

Main themes

  • contending for the faith
  • God keeps His people
  • warning

Key verse

Now unto him that is able to keep you from falling, and to present you faultless before the presence of his glory with exceeding joy,
Jude 1:24 (KJV)

How to read Jude

Read it in one or two sittings first; short letters were written to be read aloud in one go. Ask three questions as you go: what did this mean to the first readers, what does it show about God, and what does it ask of me?
