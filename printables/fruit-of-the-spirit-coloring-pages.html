Fruit of the Spirit Coloring Pages

Nine free printable pages — one fruit and one word each — for Sunday school, VBS, homeschool or a quiet Sunday afternoon.

How to use it

Each page pairs one quality from Galatians 5:22–23 with a fruit to color inside a big mandala border, so younger kids get simple shapes and older kids get the detail.

Print the whole set as a nine-week series: one fruit a week, with a short conversation about where you saw it that week.
