Screen-Free Activity Bingo

A bingo card of things to do away from screens. Every card is shuffled, so siblings can race.

How to use it

Tap “Print 6 different cards” to give everyone their own shuffled card — the squares are the same, the order is not, so there is a real race to five in a row.

Set a time limit (a weekend, a week) and a small prize. Prizes that are time together tend to beat prizes that cost money.

From the makers of FocusPass: App Blocker. Blocks the apps you choose using Apple’s Screen Time system, and grants short timed Passes when you really need access.
