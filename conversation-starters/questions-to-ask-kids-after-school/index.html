Questions to Ask Kids After School

“How was school?” “Fine.” Sound familiar? Specific, playful questions get real answers. Tap Draw for a random one at pickup or dinner.

Fun and silly

  1. What was the funniest thing that happened today?
  2. If you could swap places with anyone in your class for a day, who would it be?
  3. What did you eat for lunch, and would you give it a score out of 10?
  4. Did anyone do something really weird today?
  5. If today were a colour, what colour would it be?
  6. What game did you play at recess?
  7. If your teacher were an animal, what would they be?
  8. What's one thing you'd change about school if you were in charge?
  9. What sound did you hear the most today?
  10. Tell me something that made you laugh.

Friends and feelings

  1. Who did you sit with at lunch?
  2. Was anyone kind to you today?
  3. Were you kind to someone today?
  4. Did anything make you feel left out?
  5. Who would you like to play with more?
  6. What was the best part of your day?
  7. What was the hardest part of your day?
  8. Did you feel proud of yourself at any point?
  9. Did anything make you feel worried?
  10. Who made you smile today?

For tough days

  10. Can I tell you about a hard day I had once?

Questions

What should I ask my child instead of “How was your day?”

Ask something specific, like “What made you laugh today?” or “Who did you sit with at lunch?” Specific questions are easier to answer than broad ones.

When is the best time to ask?

Many kids open up more in the car, at bedtime or while doing something side by side, rather than face to face right after school.
