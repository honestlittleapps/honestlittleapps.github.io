Questions

What is a one word for the year?
It's a single word you choose to shape the year, in place of a list of resolutions. You come back to it when you make decisions or need to reset.
How do I choose my word for 2027?
Think about what you most needed in 2026 and who you want to be at the end of 2027. This quiz asks five questions like that and suggests one of the nine fruits of the Spirit.
What are the fruits of the Spirit?
Galatians 5:22-23 lists love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith (or faithfulness), gentleness and self-control.
