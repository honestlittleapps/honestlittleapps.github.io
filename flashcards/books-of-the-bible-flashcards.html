About these cards

The Protestant Bible has 66 books: 39 in the Old Testament (929 chapters) and 27 in the New Testament (260 chapters). Learning them in order makes every Bible study faster, because you can find any passage without the table of contents.

Each card shows where the book sits, its section (Law, History, Poetry, Prophets, Gospels, letters) and the books on either side, which is what actually makes the order stick.

How to import into Anki or Quizlet

Anki: download the .txt file, then in Anki choose File › Import. It is tab-separated; the first field becomes the front and the second the back.

Quizlet: create a set, choose Import, and paste the contents of the .txt file. Set “Between term and definition” to Tab and “Between rows” to New line.

Questions

How many books are in the Bible?

The Protestant Bible has 66 books: 39 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New Testament. Catholic and Orthodox Bibles include additional books.

What is the fastest way to memorize the books of the Bible?

Learn them in sections (Law, History, Poetry, Major and Minor Prophets, Gospels, letters) and review a few minutes a day. These flashcards repeat the ones you miss sooner, which is called spaced repetition.

Which is the longest book of the Bible?

Psalms, with 150 chapters.
