About these cards

There are 88 official constellations, recognized by the International Astronomical Union. These 31 are the ones most people learn first. Seasons are for evening skies in the Northern Hemisphere; the Southern Hemisphere cards are marked.

Questions

How many constellations are there?

88, as defined by the International Astronomical Union in 1922.

What is the easiest constellation to find?

Orion in winter (look for three stars in a row, his belt) and the Big Dipper, part of Ursa Major.

What is the brightest star in the night sky?

Sirius, in Canis Major.
