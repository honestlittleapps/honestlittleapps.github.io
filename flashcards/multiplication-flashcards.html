About these cards

Choose a table (start with the 2s, 5s and 10s), say the answer out loud, then flip. Mark it Again or Got it. Progress is saved on this device, so a few minutes a day adds up. Tricks for the 2s, 4s, 5s, 9s, 10s and 11s are on the back of the cards.

How to import into Anki or Quizlet

Anki: download the .txt file, then in Anki choose File › Import. It is tab-separated; the first field becomes the front and the second the back.

Quizlet: create a set, choose Import, and paste the contents of the .txt file. Set “Between term and definition” to Tab and “Between rows” to New line.

Questions

What order should kids learn the times tables?

Most teachers start with 2s, 5s and 10s, then 3s and 4s, then 6s–9s, and 11s and 12s last. Squares (3×3, 7×7) are worth learning as a set.

How long should multiplication practice be?

Short and daily: about 5–10 minutes. Focus on the facts your child misses.

Is there a trick for the 9 times table?

For 9×1 to 9×10, the digits of the answer add up to 9 (9×7 = 63, and 6+3 = 9).
