About these cards

An adult skeleton has 206 bones. You don’t need all of them to understand your body; these cards cover the major bones grouped by region, from the cranium to the calcaneus, plus the facts people most often get asked.

Questions

How many bones are in the human body?

An adult has 206 bones. Babies are born with roughly 270–300, and many fuse together during growth.

What is the largest bone in the body?

The femur (thigh bone) is the longest and strongest bone.

What is the smallest bone in the body?

The stapes, one of the three tiny bones in the middle ear.
