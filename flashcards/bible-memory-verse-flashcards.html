About these cards

The King James Version is in the public domain in the United States, so these cards are free to print and share for church, Sunday school and homeschool. Verses are grouped by theme so you can learn a handful for a hard week: Peace for anxiety, Courage before something new, Salvation for sharing your faith.

How to import into Anki or Quizlet

Anki: download the .txt file, then in Anki choose File › Import. It is tab-separated; the first field becomes the front and the second the back.

Quizlet: create a set, choose Import, and paste the contents of the .txt file. Set “Between term and definition” to Tab and “Between rows” to New line.

Questions

How do you memorize a Bible verse quickly?

Say the reference, say the verse, then check it. Repeat the verses you miss sooner and the ones you know later. Ten minutes a day works better than one long session.

Can I print these for my church or class?

Yes. The KJV text is public domain in the US and the cards are free for home, classroom and church use.

What version are these verses?
