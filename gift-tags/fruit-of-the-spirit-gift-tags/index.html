Questions

How do I print these gift tags?

Type the names (optional), tap Print, and choose 100% or Actual size. Card stock works best. You can also download the blank PDF and write names by hand.

What size are the tags?

Each tag is about 2.5 × 2.4 inches, twelve to a US Letter sheet. On A4 paper, choose “Fit to page”.

Yes, they're free for personal, classroom, church and nonprofit use. Please don't resell them.
