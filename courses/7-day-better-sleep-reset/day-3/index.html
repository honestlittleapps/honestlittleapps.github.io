Day 3: Use light on purpose

Bright light in the morning helps set your clock; bright light late at night pushes it later. Daylight is far brighter than indoor lighting, even on cloudy days.

Today's action

Get outside for 10 minutes within an hour of waking. Tonight, switch to lamps instead of overhead lights for the last hour before bed.
