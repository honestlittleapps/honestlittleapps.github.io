Holy Week Timeline

Follow the last week of Jesus' life day by day, with the passages to read each day. The order follows the traditional reading of the Gospels.

Palm Sunday
Jesus rides into Jerusalem on a donkey as crowds wave palm branchesMatthew 21:1–11; John 12:12–19
Monday
Jesus curses the fig tree and clears the TempleMark 11:12–19
Tuesday
Jesus teaches in the Temple and on the Mount of OlivesMark 11:20–13:37
Wednesday
Jesus is anointed at Bethany; Judas agrees to betray himMark 14:1–11
Maundy Thursday
The Last Supper, Gethsemane and Jesus' arrestMark 14:12–72; John 13–17
Good Friday
Jesus is tried, crucified and buriedMark 15; John 18–19
Holy Saturday
The tomb is sealed and guardedMatthew 27:62–66
Easter Sunday
The tomb is empty: Jesus is risenMatthew 28; Luke 24; John 20

What is quiz mode?

Quiz mode blurs each event so you can guess what happened at each date, then tap to reveal it. It works well in a classroom or small group.
