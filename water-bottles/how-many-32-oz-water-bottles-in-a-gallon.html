How the maths works

Divide your goal in fluid ounces by the bottle size. One US gallon is 128 fl oz and one liter is 33.81 fl oz. Round up if you want to be sure you hit the goal.

Questions

How many 32 oz water bottles make a gallon?

4 bottles. A US gallon is 128 fl oz, and 128 ÷ 32 = 4.

How many 32 oz bottles are 64 oz?

2 bottles.

How much water should I drink a day?

It varies with body size, activity, climate and health. The US National Academies suggest about 3.7 liters of total water a day for men and 2.7 for women, including water from food. Your doctor can advise you.
