The Wright Brothers Mini Book

The bicycle shop, kites and gliders, the wind tunnel, Kitty Hawk and the first flight on December 17, 1903. One sheet, one cut, 8 pages.

How to fold it (one cut, no glue)

  1. Print the PDF on one sheet of US Letter or A4 paper, landscape, at 100% (or "Fit to page").
  2. Fold it in half the long way, crease, and unfold. Then fold it in half the short way, and in half again. Unfold. You'll have 8 boxes.
  3. Fold it in half the short way and cut along the solid line in the middle, from the folded edge to the centre crease. Unfold.
  4. Fold it the long way again, then push the two ends toward each other so the middle opens into a plus shape. Flatten it into a book with the cover on the front.
