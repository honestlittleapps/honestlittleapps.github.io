Sun and Shadows Mini Book

Sunrise in the east, short shadows at midday, long shadows at sunset, golden hour and sun safety. One sheet, one cut, 8 pages.

How to fold it (one cut, no glue)

  1. Print the PDF on one sheet of US Letter or A4 paper, landscape, at 100% (or "Fit to page").
  2. Fold it in half the long way, crease, and unfold. Then fold it in half the short way, and in half again. Unfold. You'll have 8 boxes.
  3. Fold it in half the short way and cut along the solid line in the middle, from the folded edge to the centre crease. Unfold.
  4. Fold it the long way again, then push the two ends toward each other so the middle opens into a plus shape. Flatten it into a book with the cover on the front.

Sun and Shadows

Watch your shadow change all day
Name:

Sunrise

In the morning the sun rises in the east. Your shadow stretches long, away from the sun.

2

Climbing high

As the sun climbs higher in the sky, your shadow gets shorter.

3

Midday

Around the middle of the day the sun is at its highest, and shadows are at their shortest.

4

Afternoon

In the afternoon the sun sinks toward the west, and your shadow grows long again, pointing east.

5

Golden hour

Just after sunrise and just before sunset, the light looks warm, golden and soft. Photographers love it!

6

Stay safe

Never look straight at the sun, even with sunglasses. Draw your shadow in the morning, at noon and in the evening!

7
The end!
