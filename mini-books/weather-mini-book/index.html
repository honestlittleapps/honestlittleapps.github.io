How to fold it (one cut, no glue)

  1. Print the PDF on one sheet of US Letter or A4 paper, landscape, at 100% (or "Fit to page").
  2. Fold it in half the long way, crease, and unfold. Then fold it in half the short way, and in half again. Unfold. You'll have 8 boxes.
  3. Fold it in half the short way and cut along the solid line in the middle, from the folded edge to the centre crease. Unfold.
  4. Fold it the long way again, then push the two ends toward each other so the middle opens into a plus shape. Flatten it into a book with the cover on the front.

My Weather Book

Sun, clouds, rain, storms and snow
Name:

Sun

The sun warms the land and the air. Without it, Earth would be frozen and dark.

2

Clouds

Clouds are made of millions of tiny drops of water or bits of ice, floating in the air.

3

Rain

When the tiny drops bump together, they grow. When they get too heavy, they fall as rain.

4

Thunder and lightning

Lightning is a giant spark. Thunder is the sound it makes. When thunder roars, go indoors!

5

Snow

When it is cold enough, water in clouds freezes into snowflakes. Snowflakes have six sides.

6

Wind and rainbows

Wind is moving air. A rainbow appears when sunlight shines through raindrops, with the sun behind you.

7
The end!
