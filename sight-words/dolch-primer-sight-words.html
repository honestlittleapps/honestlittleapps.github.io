How to practice at home

  1. Five minutes, most days. Short and often beats one long session.
  2. Mix known and new words. Shuffle so your child gets quick wins between harder words.
  3. Use them in a sentence. After a word is read, say a quick sentence together with it.
  4. Move on at about 90%. When almost every word is read on sight, try the next level.

Questions

What are Dolch sight words?

A list of 220 high-frequency words compiled by Edward William Dolch and published in 1936, grouped from pre-primer to third grade. Many can’t be sounded out easily, so children learn to recognize them on sight.

How many primer sight words are there?

The Dolch primer list has 52 words, usually taught in kindergarten.
