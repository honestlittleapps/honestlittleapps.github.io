Questions

Does a reasons card actually help?

Writing your own reasons and keeping them visible is a common quit-plan step. It works best alongside a plan for cravings and support from people or programs.

Where can I get free help to quit vaping?

In the US, you can text DITCHVAPE to 88709 for the free This is Quitting program, or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW. A doctor or pharmacist can also talk through options.

Is my card uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is drawn in your browser and saved straight to your device.
