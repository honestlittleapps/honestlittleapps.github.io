Questions

How do I use the card as a lock screen?

Tap “Save lock screen”, open the image in Photos, tap Share, then “Use as Wallpaper”. The tall format fits iPhone lock screens.

What are good screen time goals for 2027?

Small, specific rules work better than vague ones: phone out of the bedroom, no apps in the first hour, or one screen-free evening a week.

Is my card uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is drawn in your browser and saved straight to your device.
