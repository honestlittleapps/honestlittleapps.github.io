Questions

What is a Christian word of the year?

It's one word you choose to focus on for the whole year, usually paired with a Bible verse, as a simple alternative to a list of resolutions.

How do I choose my word?

Pray, then pick the word that names what you most need from God or want to grow in this year. If two stand out, choose the one that feels harder.

Which translation are the verses from?

All verses are King James Version, which is public domain, so you can share the card freely.

Is my card uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is drawn in your browser and saved straight to your device.
