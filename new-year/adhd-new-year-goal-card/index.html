Questions

Why do New Year's resolutions fail with ADHD?

Long lists rely on remembering and planning, which is exactly what's hardest. One word and tiny first steps take less working memory.

What counts as a tiny first step?

Something you can start in under two minutes, like opening the document or setting a five-minute timer.

Is my card uploaded anywhere?

No. The card is drawn in your browser and saved straight to your device.
