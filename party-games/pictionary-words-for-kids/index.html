Questions

How do you play pictionary words for kids?

One player draws a secret word and sketches it, with no letters or numbers, while the others guess. Use the 60-second timer and keep score with the Got it button.

Can I print the words?

Yes. Tap Print to get the full list, then cut the words apart and put them in a bowl.
