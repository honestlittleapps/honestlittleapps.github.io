All 24 days at a glance

  1. Day 1 (Dec 1): Drink a glass of water before your first coffee.
  2. Day 2 (Dec 2): Fill a bottle and put it where you'll see it all day.
  3. Day 3 (Dec 3): Add orange slices or mint to a jug in the fridge.
  4. Day 4 (Dec 4): Have a glass of water with every meal today.
  5. Day 5 (Dec 5): Swap one sweet drink for sparkling water.
  6. Day 6 (Dec 6): Set three gentle check-ins: mid-morning, lunch, mid-afternoon.
  7. Day 7 (Dec 7): Put tape on your bottle and mark times down the side.
  8. Day 8 (Dec 8): At a party, have a glass of water between holiday drinks.
  9. Day 9 (Dec 9): Snack on something water-rich: clementines, cucumber, grapes.
  10. Day 10 (Dec 10): Warm counts too: hot water with lemon or a herbal tea.
  11. Day 11 (Dec 11): Refill your bottle every time you pass the kitchen.
  12. Day 12 (Dec 12): Pour a glass for someone else when you pour yours.
  13. Day 13 (Dec 13): Have a few sips every time you sit down today.
  14. Day 14 (Dec 14): Notice early thirst cues like dry lips or a dull headache, and drink at the first one.
  15. Day 15 (Dec 15): Take a bottle along for errands and gift shopping.
  16. Day 16 (Dec 16): Keep a glass of water on your nightstand.
  17. Day 17 (Dec 17): Freeze berries into an ice-cube tray for prettier water.
  18. Day 18 (Dec 18): Drink a glass before you leave the house.
  19. Day 19 (Dec 19): Have a glass of water before a big holiday meal.
  20. Day 20 (Dec 20): Wash your water bottle. A fresh bottle gets used.
  21. Day 21 (Dec 21): Make a pitcher of infused water for the table tonight.
  22. Day 22 (Dec 22): Travelling? Take an empty bottle through security and fill it after.
  23. Day 23 (Dec 23): Cozy evening: a mug of warm water with lemon before bed.
  24. Day 24 (Dec 24): Christmas Eve: toast with water first, then whatever comes next.

Questions

Can our office or team do this together?

Yes. Share the link in your team chat and open the door together each morning. Everyone sees the same habit that day.

How much water should I drink?

Needs vary with your body, activity and climate, so there isn't one number for everyone. Ask your doctor if you have a medical condition that affects fluids.

When do the doors open?

Each door opens on its December 2026 date, using your device's own date.
