All 24 days at a glance

  1. Day 1 (Dec 1): Tell each other one thing you noticed the other did this week that helped.
  2. Day 2 (Dec 2): Put both phones in another room for 20 minutes and drink something warm together.
  3. Day 3 (Dec 3): Ask: “What's one small thing that would make December easier for you?” Then do it.
  4. Day 4 (Dec 4): Each share a favourite holiday memory from childhood.
  5. Day 5 (Dec 5): Agree on a pause word you'll both use if a conversation starts heating up.
  6. Day 6 (Dec 6): Plan a no-cost date for next week and put it in both calendars.
  7. Day 7 (Dec 7): Ask: “When do you feel most appreciated by me?” Listen for the details.
  8. Day 8 (Dec 8): Write a three-line note and leave it somewhere they'll find it tomorrow.
  9. Day 9 (Dec 9): Take a 15-minute walk together. One rule: no logistics talk.
  10. Day 10 (Dec 10): Each name one tradition you want to keep and one you'd happily drop.
  11. Day 11 (Dec 11): Ask: “Is there something you've wanted to tell me but haven't found the moment?” Listen. Don't fix.
  12. Day 12 (Dec 12): Hug for 20 full seconds. Yes, count.
  13. Day 13 (Dec 13): Pick one stressful holiday task and split it so neither of you carries it alone.
  14. Day 14 (Dec 14): Ask: “What's a gift that isn't a thing that you'd love from me?”
  15. Day 15 (Dec 15): Tell the story of the first time you knew you liked each other. Tell it together.
  16. Day 16 (Dec 16): Practise a repair line out loud: “I want us to be on the same side. Can we start over?”
  17. Day 17 (Dec 17): Cook or order something neither of you has tried before.
  18. Day 18 (Dec 18): Ask: “What does a good day look like for you lately?”
  19. Day 19 (Dec 19): Ten-minute money check-in, kind voices only: what are we spending this month?
  20. Day 20 (Dec 20): Look through old photos together for ten minutes.
  21. Day 21 (Dec 21): Each finish the sentence: “I'm proud of us for…”
  22. Day 22 (Dec 22): Plan family visits together: when you'll leave, and a quiet signal if one of you needs a break.
  23. Day 23 (Dec 23): Each write one hope for the two of you in 2027. Swap and read.
  24. Day 24 (Dec 24): Christmas Eve: thank each other for one specific thing from this year.

Questions

Do we both need the page?

No. One of you can open the door and read it out. If you'd like it on both phones, use the Share button to send the link.

How long does each idea take?

Most take five to fifteen minutes. A few are a single question to ask over dinner.

Can we do it late or start after December 1?

Yes. Doors stay open once their date arrives, so you can catch up any time.

Is it free?

Yes, with no account or email. It's made by the team behind UnFight: Couples & Marriage.
