All 24 days at a glance

  1. Day 1 (Dec 1): Pick tonight's lights-down time and write it on a sticky note by the bed.
  2. Day 2 (Dec 2): Dim every light you can, an hour before bed.
  3. Day 3 (Dec 3): Write tomorrow's top three tasks so they don't follow you into bed.
  4. Day 4 (Dec 4): Charge your phone outside the bedroom tonight.
  5. Day 5 (Dec 5): Take a warm shower or bath one to two hours before bed.
  6. Day 6 (Dec 6): Breathe in for 4, out for 6. Ten slow rounds.
  7. Day 7 (Dec 7): Read ten pages of a paper book instead of scrolling.
  8. Day 8 (Dec 8): Make the bedroom a little cooler tonight.
  9. Day 9 (Dec 9): No caffeine after lunch today.
  10. Day 10 (Dec 10): Five minutes of gentle stretching: neck, shoulders, calves.
  11. Day 11 (Dec 11): Make tomorrow's first ten minutes easy: lay out clothes or set up the coffee.
  12. Day 12 (Dec 12): Write one worry and one next step for it. Then close the notebook.
  13. Day 13 (Dec 13): Listen to one calm song with your eyes closed.
  14. Day 14 (Dec 14): Get up at your usual time tomorrow, even after a late night.
  15. Day 15 (Dec 15): Step outside for ten minutes of morning daylight tomorrow.
  16. Day 16 (Dec 16): Clear one surface in your bedroom.
  17. Day 17 (Dec 17): Write down three good things about today.
  18. Day 18 (Dec 18): Swap one scroll session for a podcast or quiet music.
  19. Day 19 (Dec 19): Eat dinner a little earlier tonight if you can.
  20. Day 20 (Dec 20): Body scan: notice your feet, legs, belly, hands, shoulders, then your face.
  21. Day 21 (Dec 21): Longest night of the year: switch on a warm lamp and name one thing that got you through 2026.
  22. Day 22 (Dec 22): Set a holiday nap rule: short (about 20 minutes) and before mid-afternoon.
  23. Day 23 (Dec 23): Make a wind-down kit: cozy socks, a book, water, a small lamp.
  24. Day 24 (Dec 24): Christmas Eve: put the to-do list away. Whatever isn't done can wait.

Questions

When should I open the door?

In the evening, ideally an hour or so before bed, so you have time to try the idea tonight.

Is this medical advice?

No. These are general wind-down habits. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, talk to a doctor.

Why does door 21 mention the longest night?

December 21, 2026 is the winter solstice in the Northern Hemisphere, the longest night of the year.
