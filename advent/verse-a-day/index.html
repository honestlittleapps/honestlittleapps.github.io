How the Advent verse calendar works

Advent is the four weeks of waiting before Christmas. This calendar walks from the prophets (Isaiah and Micah) to Mary and the angels, and on to the manger in Luke 2. Each door opens on its own date, so there's always one to look forward to. Your opened doors are saved on this device, nowhere else.

Scripture is from the World English Bible (WEB), which is in the public domain.

Questions

When does Advent start in 2026?
Advent 2026 begins on Sunday, November 29. This calendar uses the familiar December 1 to 24 doors, so it fits any Advent wreath or chocolate calendar.
Is this Advent calendar free?
Yes. No account, no email and no ads. Your progress stays in your own browser.
Can our church or family use it together?
Yes. Share the link, and everyone opens the same door each day. Each door also has a Share button.
Which Bible translation is used?
The World English Bible (WEB), a modern public-domain translation.
