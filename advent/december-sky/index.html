What's in the December 2026 sky

December has the year's earliest sunsets (around December 5 to 10 at mid-northern latitudes), the Geminid meteor shower on the night of December 13 to 14 with only a thin crescent Moon, the winter solstice on December 21 at 20:50 UTC, and a full Cold Moon on the evening of December 23 in the Americas. Venus is the morning star all month. Moon times and the solstice were calculated for 2026, and the Geminid and planet notes are for the Northern Hemisphere.

Your location is only used in your browser to work out your sunset time, and it's never sent anywhere.

Questions

When is the Geminid meteor shower in 2026?
The Geminids peak on the night of December 13 to 14, 2026. The crescent Moon sets in the mid-evening, so the hours after 10 pm are dark and good for watching.
When is the winter solstice in 2026?
December 21, 2026, at 20:50 UTC (3:50 pm Eastern). It's the shortest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere.
Why is the earliest sunset before the solstice?
Solar noon drifts later through December because of Earth's tilted axis and its elliptical orbit. So, around 40° north, the earliest sunset comes around December 5 to 10, while the latest sunrise is in early January.
When is the full moon in December 2026?
The Cold Moon is full at 01:28 UTC on December 24, which is the evening of December 23 in North America.
