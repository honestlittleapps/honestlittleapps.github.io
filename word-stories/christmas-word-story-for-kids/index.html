Questions

How do you play a fill-in-the-blank story?

One person asks for each type of word (a noun, a verb, a colour) without reading the story. Then you read the finished story out loud.

Is this good for grammar practice?

Yes. Kids practise nouns, verbs and adjectives while laughing at the result.

Can I print it?

Yes. Print the blank version and fill it in with a pencil.
