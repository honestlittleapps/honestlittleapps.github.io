Tips for better sleep at this age

  • Wake at the same time every day; it anchors your body clock.
  • Get daylight soon after waking and keep lights low in the last hour before bed.
  • If poor sleep, loud snoring or daytime sleepiness last for weeks, talk to a doctor.

These ranges are from the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, based on the American Academy of Sleep Medicine and Sleep Research Society. Needs vary from person to person.

Questions

How much sleep does someone over 65 need?

7–8 hours per 24 hours, according to the CDC.

What if my child sleeps less than this?

Occasional short nights are normal. If you regularly sleep poorly or feel very sleepy in the day, talk to a doctor.
