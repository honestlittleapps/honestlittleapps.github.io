Questions

Why is the King James Bible hard to read?

It was translated in 1611, and some words have since changed meaning or fallen out of use. Learning a few dozen of them makes it much easier.

What does thou mean?

“Thou” means “you” when speaking to one person. “Ye” is the plural.
