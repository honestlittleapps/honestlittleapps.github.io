What does “Fain” mean in the Bible?

Fain = gladly, eagerly

“And he would fain have filled his belly with the husks that the swine did eat.”
Luke 15:16, King James Version

Explanation

“Fain” means “gladly” or “would have liked to”.

Questions

What does fain mean in the KJV?

In the King James Version, “Fain” means gladly, eagerly. “Fain” means “gladly” or “would have liked to”.

Why does the King James Bible use old words?

The King James Version was published in 1611, and English has changed since then. Some words have dropped out of use and others have changed meaning.
