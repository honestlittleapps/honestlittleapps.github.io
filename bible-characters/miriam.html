Who Was Miriam in the Bible?

The short version, the key verse, and three quiz questions to check what you remember.

Miriam was Moses’ sister, a prophetess and worship leader.

As a girl, Miriam watched over baby Moses in the Nile and arranged for his own mother to nurse him. After Israel crossed the Red Sea, she led the women in song with a timbrel. She is called a prophetess and later spoke against Moses and was struck with leprosy until he prayed for her.

Key verse

And Miriam answered them, Sing ye to the LORD , for he hath triumphed gloriously; the horse and his rider hath he thrown into the sea.
Exodus 15:21 (KJV)

Where to read about Miriam

Exodus 2; 15; Numbers 12; Micah 6:4

Quick quiz

1. What instrument did Miriam play after the Red Sea crossing?

A timbrel (Exodus 15:20).

2. Who were Miriam’s brothers?

Moses and Aaron.

3. What title does Exodus 15:20 give Miriam?

Prophetess.
