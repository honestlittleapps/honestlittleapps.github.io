Who Was Deborah in the Bible?

The short version, the key verse, and three quiz questions to check what you remember.

Deborah was a prophetess and judge of Israel.

Deborah judged Israel under a palm tree. She called Barak to lead the army against Sisera, and when he insisted she come, she went with him. Israel won the battle, and Deborah and Barak sang a victory song.

Key verse

And Deborah said unto Barak, Up; for this is the day in which the LORD hath delivered Sisera into thine hand: is not the LORD gone out before thee? So Barak went down from mount Tabor, and ten thousand men after him.
Judges 4:14 (KJV)

Where to read about Deborah

Judges 4–5

Quick quiz

3. Who killed Sisera?
