Who Was Jonah in the Bible?

The short version, the key verse, and three quiz questions to check what you remember.

Jonah was the prophet swallowed by a great fish.

God sent Jonah to warn Nineveh, but he fled by ship toward Tarshish. In a storm he was thrown overboard and swallowed by a great fish for three days and nights. He then preached in Nineveh, the city repented, and God taught the angry Jonah about His mercy.

Key verse

But I will sacrifice unto thee with the voice of thanksgiving; I will pay that that I have vowed. Salvation is of the LORD .
Jonah 2:9 (KJV)

Where to read about Jonah

Jonah 1–4; Matthew 12:40

Quick quiz

1. Which city did God send Jonah to?

3. Where was Jonah trying to go instead?
