Morning Prayers for Men

Before the phone, the inbox and the news, take two minutes. These are short, direct prayers for men who want to start the day pointed the right way.

Before the day startsFor workFor my familyFor strength and integrityOne-line prayers for the commute

Try praying one before you look at your phone. Most men find that's the hardest and most useful part.

Before the day starts

Lord, this day is Yours before it's mine. Lead me where You want me, keep me from what pulls me off course, and make me useful to someone today. Amen.

Father, thank You for waking me up. Give me a clear head, a steady heart and the discipline to do the next right thing, even when it's hard. Amen.

God, I don't want to drift through today. Show me what matters, help me ignore what doesn't, and give me the courage to act. Amen.

Lord, before I check anything else, I check in with You. Set my priorities, guard my words and keep my eyes on what's good. Amen.

For work

Lord, bless the work of my hands today (Psalm 90:17). Help me work honestly, treat people fairly and do my job as if I were doing it for You. Amen.

Father, give me patience with difficult people and wisdom with hard decisions. Let me be the kind of man others can trust. Amen.

God, provide for my family through my work. Help me be faithful with what You've given me, big or small. Amen.

For my family

Lord, protect my wife and children today. Help me lead with love, not anger, and come home with energy left for them. Amen.

Father, make me a man my kids can look up to, not because I'm perfect, but because I'm honest about where I'm not. Amen.

God, help me listen more than I talk at home tonight. Show me what my family needs from me. Amen.

For strength and integrity

Lord, I want to be the same man in private as I am in public. Give me strength to resist what I know is wrong today. Amen.

Father, “Watch ye, stand fast in the faith, quit you like men, be strong” (1 Corinthians 16:13). Make that true of me today. Amen.

God, when I'm tempted to take the easy way, remind me who I want to become. Amen.

One-line prayers for the commute

Lord, make me patient in traffic and kind in conversations.

Father, help me finish what I start today.

God, guard my tongue.

Jesus, lead me, and I'll follow.

Lord, let someone see You in me today.

Questions

What is a good morning prayer for men?

Keep it short and specific: thank God for the day, ask for wisdom and strength, and pray for your family and your work. The prayers above take under a minute each.

How do I build a morning prayer habit?

Pray before you look at your phone, at the same time and place each day. Starting with one short prayer is better than a long one you skip.

Can I share these with a men's group?

Yes. They're free to copy and share.
