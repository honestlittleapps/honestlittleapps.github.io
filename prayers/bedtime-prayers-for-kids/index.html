Questions

What is a simple bedtime prayer for a child?

“Dear God, thank You for today. Keep me safe all through the night, and wake me with the morning light. Amen.” Short rhyming prayers are easy for young children to remember.

At what age can kids pray on their own?

Many children start repeating simple prayers around age two or three, and can make up their own by five or six. Praying together helps them learn.
