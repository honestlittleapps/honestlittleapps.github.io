How the numbers are worked out

Hours a day × 365 days. Full days = total hours ÷ 24. Waking days assume 16 waking hours. The comparisons use round, stated assumptions: a 300-page book takes about 6 hours to read, a feature film runs 2 hours, and a 5 km walk takes about 1 hour. These are estimates for perspective, not a judgement.

Questions

How many hours a year do I spend on my phone?
Multiply your daily screen time by 365. At 4.5 hours a day, that's about 1,643 hours, or roughly 68 full days a year.
Where do I find my screen time on iPhone?
Go to Settings → Screen Time → See All App & Website Activity, then switch to Week to see your daily average.
What's a realistic screen time goal for 2027?
Cut 30 minutes a day rather than aiming for a dramatic drop. That alone gives back over 180 hours a year.
Is my number saved anywhere?
No. The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent anywhere.
