Questions

What should I buy before a winter storm?

Water, food that needs no cooking, flashlights and batteries, a charged power bank, warm blankets, any medicines you need, and ice melt or sand.

Is it safe to run a generator in the garage?

No. Generators, grills and camp stoves give off carbon monoxide and must only be used outdoors, away from windows, doors and vents.

Where do these recommendations come from?

They follow common public-safety guidance such as Ready.gov. Always follow instructions from your local officials.
