Questions

How many exits should a fire escape plan have?

Plan two ways out of every room, usually a door and a window.

How often should we practise a fire drill?

Fire safety groups recommend practising your home fire drill at least twice a year.

Where should our meeting spot be?

Somewhere outside that won't move and is a safe distance from the house, like a neighbour's mailbox or a tree.
