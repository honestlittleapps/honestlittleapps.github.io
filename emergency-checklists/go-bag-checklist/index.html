Questions

What goes in a 72-hour go bag?

Water and food for three days, copies of important documents, medicines, a flashlight, a power bank, a radio, a change of clothes and some cash.

How often should I check my go bag?

Check it every six months. Swap out food and water, update documents and replace expired medicines.

Where should I keep it?

Somewhere you can grab it on the way out, like a hall closet or by the door.
