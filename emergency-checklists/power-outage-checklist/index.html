Questions

How long does food last in the fridge during a power outage?

About 4 hours if the door stays closed. A full freezer holds its temperature for about 48 hours, or about 24 hours if half full.

Should I use candles during a power outage?

Flashlights are safer. Candles are a common cause of house fires during outages.

What should I do first when the power goes out?

Grab a flashlight, check if neighbours have power, report the outage and keep the fridge and freezer closed.
