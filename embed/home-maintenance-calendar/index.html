Questions

Does it update by itself?

Yes. The widget shows the current entry every day and updates automatically. You never touch it again after pasting the code.

Where does it work?

Anywhere that accepts an iframe or embed code: WordPress (Custom HTML block), Squarespace (Code block), Wix (Embed code), Google Sites (Embed › By code), Webflow, Ghost, and plain HTML pages.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no sign-up, no ads in the widget and no tracking cookies. A small credit link at the bottom points to the free calendar and the related app.

Can I make it dark?

Yes. Add ?theme=dark to the end of the widget address in the code.
