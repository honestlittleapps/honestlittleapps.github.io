# Sunset and golden hour times (open data)

Daily sunset, golden hour (sun from 6° above to 4° below the horizon) and civil dusk for 10 cities, Nov 2026 – Dec 2027, in local time.
Computed astronomically (Astral library) for each city centre at sea level.

Cities: New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Denver, Seattle, Miami, Toronto, London. One CSV per city.

- Live iPhone calendar per city: https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/sunset-golden-hour-calendar/
- Website widget (pick a city): https://honestlittleapps.github.io/embed/sunset-golden-hour-calendar/
- Related app: [Asterfold: Sunset Planner](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6806044403?pt=129123876&ct=gh-sunset&mt=8), which shows where the sun sets from your exact viewpoint

Note: the golden-hour start is the time the sun drops to 6°; real light depends on terrain and weather.
