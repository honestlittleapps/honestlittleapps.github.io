# US payday and tax calendar 2027 (open data)

- `biweekly-paydays-2027-schedule-a.csv`: every other Friday from Jan 8, 2027 (26 paydays)
- `biweekly-paydays-2027-schedule-b.csv`: every other Friday from Jan 15, 2027 (26 paydays)
- `us-federal-holidays-2027-observed.csv`: federal holidays, with both the actual date and the observed weekday where they differ
- `us-contractor-tax-dates-2027.csv`: federal estimated-tax and 1099-NEC dates, moved off weekends and holidays. Always confirm at irs.gov.

Live calendars: https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/biweekly-payday-calendar-2027/ and https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/contractor-tax-and-invoice-calendar-2027/

Related apps: [EarnCheck: Shift & Pay Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6798048163?pt=129123876&ct=gh-payday&mt=8) (compare shifts with your pay stub) and [ScopeBid: Estimate & Invoice](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6796729890?pt=129123876&ct=gh-payday&mt=8) (estimates and invoices for independent pros).

Not tax advice.
