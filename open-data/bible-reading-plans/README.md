# Bible reading plans (open data)

Free, ready-to-use Bible reading plans and trivia in CSV, for church websites, small groups, apps and spreadsheets.
Translation-neutral: references and short paraphrases only, no copyrighted Bible text.

| File | What it is | Free calendar | Related app |
|---|---|---|---|
| `proverbs-a-day.csv` | Proverbs 1–31 matched to the day of the month, with a one-line theme and a practical challenge | [Subscribe](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/proverbs-a-day-for-men/) | [FORGED: Daily Bible for Men](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6794772046?pt=129123876&ct=gh-bible-plans&mt=8) |
| `psalms-in-a-month.csv` | All 150 Psalms in 30 days (day + 30n), Psalm 119 on the 31st | [Subscribe](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/psalms-in-a-month/) | [Lampstep: Bible for Real Life](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6820775927?pt=129123876&ct=gh-bible-plans&mt=8) |
| `new-testament-2027-weekdays.csv` | All 260 New Testament chapters on the 261 weekdays of 2027 | [Subscribe](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/new-testament-in-a-year-2027/) | [Wayfold: Understand the Bible](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6820304462?pt=129123876&ct=gh-bible-plans&mt=8) |
| `fruit-of-the-spirit-weekly.csv` | Galatians 5:22–23, one fruit per week, with Greek word and key verse | [Subscribe](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/fruit-of-the-spirit-weekly/) | [Ninefruit: Fruit of the Spirit](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6820778195?pt=129123876&ct=gh-bible-plans&mt=8) |
| `bible-trivia.csv` | 31 family-friendly Bible trivia questions with answers and references | [Subscribe](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/bible-trivia-question-of-the-day/) | [Scrollway: Bible Study Game](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6821770165?pt=129123876&ct=gh-bible-plans&mt=8) |

Every plan is also available as a live iPhone/Google/Outlook calendar subscription, a [daily page](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/today/), an RSS feed and a [free website widget](https://honestlittleapps.github.io/embed/).
