# Full moons, equinoxes and solstices 2026–2027 (open data)

Every full moon from late November 2026 through January 2028 with its traditional North American folk name, plus each equinox and solstice. Computed with PyEphem.
The CSV gives UTC dates. The live calendar (https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/full-moon-calendar-2027/) has the exact minute, shown in each viewer's time zone.

Related app: [Puffgrove: Grove Creatures](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6823566932?pt=129123876&ct=gh-moons&mt=8)
