Grocery Store Math Scavenger Hunt

15 math and reading missions to do on a normal shopping trip. Tick items off right here on your phone, or print the sheet.

⬇ Download the printable PDF one page · free

Found: 0 / 15

  • Find something that costs less than $1
  • Find two items whose prices add up to about $5
  • Find a price ending in .99 and round it
  • Find a box with more than 10 things inside
  • Find the heaviest bag of apples you're allowed to lift
  • Find a word with "ea" on a sign
  • Count the checkout lanes
  • Find something sold by the pound
  • Find a sign with a number bigger than 100
  • Find the cheaper of two sizes of the same thing (check the unit price)
  • Read an ingredient list with 5 or fewer ingredients
  • Find a shape that is a cylinder
  • Estimate the total of 3 items before checkout
  • Find a word that rhymes with "bread"
  • Find something that comes in a dozen

Grown-ups: let them hold the list and the pencil.
