"Thy Word Is a Lamp" Cross-Stitch Pattern

A free KJV Psalm 119:105 cross-stitch chart in black with a gold border. Beginner friendly: full stitches only.

You'll need

  • ■ DMC 310 Black
  • ■ DMC 725 Topaz
  • 14-count Aida (or 11-count for a bigger piece)
  • Size 24 tapestry needle and a hoop

How to stitch it

  1. Cut your Aida about 3 inches bigger than the design on every side and fold it in quarters to find the centre.
  2. Start at the centre arrows on the chart and work full cross stitches with 2 strands.
  3. Finish one colour block at a time, then press from the back and frame.
