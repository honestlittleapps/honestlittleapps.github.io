Try each one before you peek. Answers shown: 0 of 20

1. What has hands but can't clap?

A clock.

2. What has legs but doesn't walk?

A table.

3. What has to be broken before you can use it?

An egg.

4. What gets bigger the more you take away?

A hole.

5. What has an eye but can't see?

A needle.

6. What has words but never speaks?

A book.

7. What kind of band never plays music?

A rubber band.

8. What has a thumb and four fingers but isn't alive?

A glove.

9. What goes up when rain comes down?

An umbrella.

10. I'm tall when I'm young and short when I'm old. What am I?

A candle.

11. What can you catch but not throw?

A cold.

12. What has lots of keys but can't open doors?

A piano.

13. What room has no doors or windows?

A mushroom.

14. What has a bottom at the top?

Your legs.

15. What month has 28 days?

All of them.

16. What can you hear but not touch or see?

Your voice.

17. What is full of holes but still holds water?

A sponge.

18. What building has the most stories?

A library.

19. What do you call a bear with no teeth?

A gummy bear.

20. Why did the math book look sad?

Because it had too many problems.
