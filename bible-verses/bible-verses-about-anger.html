Bible Verses About Anger

Scripture for heated moments and hard conversations.

10 verses for heated moments and hard conversations, in the King James Version. Tap Copy to send one to a friend, or print the page for your fridge, journal or small group.

Be ye angry, and sin not: let not the sun go down upon your wrath:
Ephesians 4:26 (KJV)
Wherefore, my beloved brethren, let every man be swift to hear, slow to speak, slow to wrath: For the wrath of man worketh not the righteousness of God.
James 1:19–20 (KJV)
A soft answer turneth away wrath: but grievous words stir up anger.
Proverbs 15:1 (KJV)
A fool uttereth all his mind: but a wise man keepeth it in till afterwards.
Proverbs 29:11 (KJV)
Be not hasty in thy spirit to be angry: for anger resteth in the bosom of fools.
Ecclesiastes 7:9 (KJV)
But now ye also put off all these; anger, wrath, malice, blasphemy, filthy communication out of your mouth.
Colossians 3:8 (KJV)
He that is slow to wrath is of great understanding: but he that is hasty of spirit exalteth folly.
Proverbs 14:29 (KJV)
Cease from anger, and forsake wrath: fret not thyself in any wise to do evil.
Psalm 37:8 (KJV)
He that is slow to anger is better than the mighty; and he that ruleth his spirit than he that taketh a city.
Proverbs 16:32 (KJV)
Let all bitterness, and wrath, and anger, and clamour, and evil speaking, be put away from you, with all malice: And be ye kind one to another, tenderhearted, forgiving one another, even as God for Christ’s sake hath forgiven you.
Ephesians 4:31–32 (KJV)

How to use these verses this week

  1. Pick one. Choose the verse that hits closest to what you are facing today.

  3. Pray it back. Turn the verse into a sentence to God in your own words.
