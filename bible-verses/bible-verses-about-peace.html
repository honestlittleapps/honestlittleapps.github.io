Bible Verses About Peace

Scripture for a calm heart in a noisy week.

10 verses for a calm heart in a noisy week, in the King James Version. Tap Copy to send one to a friend, or print the page for your fridge, journal or small group.

Peace I leave with you, my peace I give unto you: not as the world giveth, give I unto you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid.
John 14:27 (KJV)
Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.
Isaiah 26:3 (KJV)
And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.
Philippians 4:7 (KJV)
Now the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing, that ye may abound in hope, through the power of the Holy Ghost.
Romans 15:13 (KJV)
The LORD bless thee, and keep thee: The LORD make his face shine upon thee, and be gracious unto thee: The LORD lift up his countenance upon thee, and give thee peace.
Numbers 6:24–26 (KJV)
I will both lay me down in peace, and sleep: for thou, LORD , only makest me dwell in safety.
Psalm 4:8 (KJV)
And let the peace of God rule in your hearts, to the which also ye are called in one body; and be ye thankful.
Colossians 3:15 (KJV)
Therefore being justified by faith, we have peace with God through our Lord Jesus Christ:
Romans 5:1 (KJV)
Now the Lord of peace himself give you peace always by all means. The Lord be with you all.
2 Thessalonians 3:16 (KJV)
Blessed are the peacemakers: for they shall be called the children of God.
Matthew 5:9 (KJV)

How to use these verses this week

  1. Pick one. Choose the verse that hits closest to what you are facing today.
  2. Write it down. Put it on your phone’s lock screen or a sticky note where you’ll see it.
  3. Pray it back. Turn the verse into a sentence to God in your own words.
  4. Learn it by heart. Our free memory verse flashcards make it easier.
