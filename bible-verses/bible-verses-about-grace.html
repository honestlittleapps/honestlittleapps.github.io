Bible Verses About Grace

Scripture about undeserved kindness from God.

10 verses about undeserved kindness from God, in the King James Version. Tap Copy to send one to a friend, or print the page for your fridge, journal or small group.

For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.
Ephesians 2:8–9 (KJV)
And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.
2 Corinthians 12:9 (KJV)
For the grace of God that bringeth salvation hath appeared to all men,
Titus 2:11 (KJV)
For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God; Being justified freely by his grace through the redemption that is in Christ Jesus:
Romans 3:23–24 (KJV)
Let us therefore come boldly unto the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy, and find grace to help in time of need.
Hebrews 4:16 (KJV)
And of his fulness have all we received, and grace for grace. For the law was given by Moses, but grace and truth came by Jesus Christ.
John 1:16–17 (KJV)
Moreover the law entered, that the offence might abound. But where sin abounded, grace did much more abound:
Romans 5:20 (KJV)
And God is able to make all grace abound toward you; that ye, always having all sufficiency in all things , may abound to every good work:
2 Corinthians 9:8 (KJV)
But the God of all grace, who hath called us unto his eternal glory by Christ Jesus, after that ye have suffered a while, make you perfect, stablish, strengthen, settle you .
1 Peter 5:10 (KJV)
For sin shall not have dominion over you: for ye are not under the law, but under grace.
Romans 6:14 (KJV)

Questions

What is the best Bible verse about grace?

Many people turn to Ephesians 2:8–9: “For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: Not of works, lest any man should boast.”
