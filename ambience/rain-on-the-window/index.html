Rain on the Window for your TV

Steady rain running down the glass with a low rumble: good for focus or sleep. It runs live in your browser, so it never loops or buffers, and there's a sleep timer for the sound.

Open full screen

Put it on your TV

  1. Smart TV, Fire TV or game console: open the TV's web browser, go to this page, tap Open full screen, then tap to start sound.
  2. From an iPhone, iPad or Mac: open it in Safari and AirPlay the screen to your Apple TV.
  3. Laptop to TV: plug in HDMI, open the scene and press Full screen.
  4. Use the sleep timer to fade the sound out after 30 minutes, an hour or two hours.
