Draw a card right now

No printer? Pass the phone around the table. Cards don't repeat until you've seen them all.

How to use them

  1. Split into two teams. Read a card aloud; the other team answers.
  2. Correct answer: 1 point. Find the reference in a Bible within 60 seconds: 1 bonus point.
  3. First team to 10 wins. Great as a 15-minute youth-group warm-up.

Questions

Do the cards have answers?

Yes. Each card prints the answer upside down at the bottom, with a Bible reference where one applies.

What ages are these Bible trivia cards for?

Most questions suit ages 8 and up, which makes them good for families, Sunday school and youth groups.

Which Bible translation is used?

Wording that quotes Scripture follows the King James Version.
