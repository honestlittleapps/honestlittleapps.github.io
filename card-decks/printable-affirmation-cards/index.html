How to use them

  1. Keep the deck by your mirror, bed or desk and draw one card each morning.
  2. Tuck a card into a lunchbox, a book or a friend's card.
  3. Pick one card for the week and write it somewhere you'll see it.

Questions

How do I print affirmation cards?

Download the PDF and print on card stock at 100% scale. Each sheet holds nine cards. Cut along the guides.

Can I give these as a gift?

Yes. They're free for personal gifts, classrooms, therapy waiting rooms and nonprofit groups. Please don't sell them.

Are these good for kids?

Most cards suit teens and adults. Many work for younger kids too, but have a grown-up pick the set.
