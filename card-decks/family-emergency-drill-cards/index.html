No printer? Pass the phone around the table. Cards don't repeat until you've seen them all.

How to use them

  1. Draw one card at dinner once or twice a week.
  2. Do it, don't just talk about it: walk to the shut-off valve, test the flashlight.
  3. Fix any gap you find within a week, then put the card at the back of the deck.

Questions

How often should families practise emergency drills?

Many families do a short drill every month or two, plus a full fire drill at least twice a year. Short, regular practice is easier to remember.

Are these cards suitable for kids?

Yes. They're written so kids can read and act on them with a grown-up. Some scenarios mention fires and storms, so pick the cards that suit your children.

Do these replace official guidance?

No. Follow your local emergency services' advice for your area. These cards help your family practise.
