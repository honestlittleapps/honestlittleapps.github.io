How to use them

  1. Light cards are for any night. Take turns drawing.
  2. Closer cards are for a slower evening. Answer, then ask one follow-up question.
  3. Repair cards are for a calm moment after a disagreement, never in the middle of one.
  4. Pass on any card, no explanation needed.

Questions

How do I print the cards?

Download the PDF and print on US Letter card stock at 100% scale. Each page holds nine poker-size cards (2.5 × 3.5 in). Print the backs page on the reverse if you'd like matching backs, then cut along the guides.

Are these good for date night?

Yes. Start with the Light cards for an easy date night, then move to Closer when you have more time.

Can couples in a rough patch use them?

The Repair cards are written for calm moments after a disagreement. If things feel unsafe, please reach out to a counsellor or a local support line rather than relying on cards.
