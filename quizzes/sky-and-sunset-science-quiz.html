All questions and answers

1Which colours does air scatter the most?Blue and violet
2What is the scattering of light by air molecules called?Rayleigh scattering
3Why are sunsets orange and red?Light crosses more air, so blue scatters away
4White sunlight is a mix of...All the colours of the rainbow
5When is golden hour?Just before sunset and after sunrise
6What is blue hour?Twilight just after sunset or before sunrise
7Which clouds often make the most colourful sunsets?Mid- and high-level clouds
8In which direction does the Sun set?Roughly west
9About how long does sunlight take to reach Earth?About 8 minutes
10A prism splits white light into colours by...Refraction
11Which visible colour has the longest wavelength?Red
12Why do clouds look white?Their droplets scatter all colours about equally
