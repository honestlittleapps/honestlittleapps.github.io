Home maintenance: January checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): · Replace or check the HVAC filter. · Test the sump pump if you have one. · Check for drafts around doors and windows.

Home maintenance: April checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): · Clean gutters and downspouts. · Check outdoor faucets and hoses for freeze damage. · Inspect deck and steps for loose boards.
03

Home maintenance: July checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): · Check the water heater area for leaks. · Re-caulk any gaps around windows. · Inspect the garage door's auto-reverse.
04

Home maintenance: October checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): · Drain and disconnect outdoor hoses before frost. · Check night lights along hallways and stairs. · Have the chimney inspected if you use it.
