Money Fact of the Day for break rooms screens

A full-screen slide that changes every day on its own. Put it on a lobby TV, classroom board, break-room screen or digital sign and forget about it. Free, no sign-up.

Put it on a screen

  1. Smart TV or Fire TV: open the TV's web browser, go to the slide address below, and make it full screen.
  2. Digital signage (Yodeck, ScreenCloud, OptiSigns, Rise Vision and similar): add a "Web page" or "URL" app and paste the address.
  3. Raspberry Pi or old laptop: run chromium --kiosk https://honestlittleapps.github.io/screens/money-fact-of-the-day-calendar/slide.html.
  4. ProPresenter or a slideshow: add a web/browser layer with the address.

Slide address: https://honestlittleapps.github.io/screens/money-fact-of-the-day-calendar/slide.html
