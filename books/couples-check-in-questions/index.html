13 Couples Check-In Questions

A 15-minute Sunday ritual. Phones down. Each person answers, the other just listens. No fixing yet. One question a week for a quarter.

Everything inside

  1. What's one thing I did this week that you appreciated?
  2. Is there anything you've been holding back from saying?
  3. What's coming up next week that we should plan together?
  4. When did you feel most connected to me this week?
  5. Is there a small argument we never finished? Let's finish it calmly.
  6. What's one way I can make your week easier?
  7. What are we looking forward to together this month?
  8. How are we doing with money, and is there anything to talk about?
  9. What's something new we could try together?
  10. Did either of us feel unheard this week?
  11. What's a habit of mine that's quietly helping us?
  12. How's your stress level, 1 to 10? What would lower it by one?
  13. What's one memory from this year you want to keep?
