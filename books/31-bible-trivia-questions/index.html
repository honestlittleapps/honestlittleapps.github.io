How many books are in the Protestant Bible?

Answer: 66: 39 in the Old Testament and 27 in the New.

Who was thrown into a den of lions?

Answer: Daniel (Daniel 6).

What is the shortest verse in most English Bibles?

Answer: John 11:35, "Jesus wept."
