Everything inside

  1. Cook something neither of you has made before.
  2. Sunset walk and takeaway somewhere with a view.
  3. Board games or cards, with a small bet (loser makes breakfast).
  4. Visit a free museum or gallery evening.
  5. Picnic in a park, even if it's just sandwiches.
  6. Try a class together: pottery, climbing, dancing.
  7. Outdoor movie or a film at home under blankets in the garden.
  8. Farmers' market morning and cook what you buy.
  9. Go back to the place of your first date.
  10. Apple picking, a pumpkin patch or a fall drive.
  11. Make a playlist for each other and swap.
  12. Walk around the holiday lights with a hot drink.
