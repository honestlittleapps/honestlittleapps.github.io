Couples Quiz Night: how well do you know each other?

30 light questions for couples' game night or a date at home. One partner answers in secret, the other guesses, and the TV keeps score for every couple.

Set up the game

Set up (30 seconds)

How to host

  1. Open this page on the computer connected to the projector or TV, or AirPlay it from an iPhone or iPad.
  2. Press Start. It goes full screen.
  3. One partner writes their real answer, the other writes their guess. Reveal both; a match scores a point for that couple.
  4. Keys: T timer · Space reveal · → next · Esc exit. The + and − buttons keep score.
  5. At the end, the winners' screen shows a QR code to the app and lets you download a winners' certificate to print or post.

Good for

Couples' game nights, small groups, marriage retreats and date night at home.

A few of the questions

  • What's my go-to comfort food?
  • What would I do with a free Saturday and no plans?
  • What's the first thing I'd grab in a fire (after people and pets)?
  • What's my most-used emoji?
  • Which song do I always sing along to?
  • What's my dream vacation spot?
