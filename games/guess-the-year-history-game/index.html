Guess the Year: a history game for the classroom projector

Each slide is a real moment from history. Teams write down the year; closest team wins the point. Then use the detective question to dig deeper.

Set up the game

Set up (30 seconds)

How to host

  1. Open this page on the computer connected to the projector or TV, or AirPlay it from an iPhone or iPad.
  2. Press Start. It goes full screen.
  3. Teams write a year on paper or a whiteboard. Reveal, then give the point to the closest team (two points for an exact hit).
  4. Keys: T timer · Space reveal · → next · Esc exit. The + and − buttons keep score.
  5. At the end, the winners' screen shows a QR code to the app and lets you download a winners' certificate to print or post.

Good for

History and social studies classes, homeschool co-ops and trivia nights.

A few of the questions

  • Abraham Lincoln delivers the Gettysburg Address. What year? (1863)
  • The first Nobel Prizes are awarded. What year? (1901)
  • The Boston Tea Party. What year? (1773)
  • The Wright brothers make the first powered, controlled flight at Kitty Hawk. What year? (1903)
  • Steve Jobs unveils the first iPhone in San Francisco. What year? (2007)
  • The British Museum opens to the public in London. What year? (1759)
