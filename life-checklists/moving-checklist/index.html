Questions

When should I start a moving checklist?

Ideally six to eight weeks before moving day, so address changes, records and utilities aren't rushed.

What goes in a first-night box?

Sheets, towels, phone chargers, medicines, basic toiletries, toilet paper, snacks, a few dishes and a change of clothes for everyone.
