Questions

How do I reset after the holidays?

Start with sleep and food, then tackle the house in short sessions, deal with returns and bills, and plan just the first week back.

I have ADHD. How do I get started?

Pick one task you can finish in five minutes, set a timer, and start there. Momentum matters more than order.
