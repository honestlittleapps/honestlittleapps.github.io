Number bonds to ten are the foundation of mental addition. Once a child knows 7 + 3 makes 10, they can work out 7 + 6 by bridging through ten: 7 + 3 = 10, and 3 more makes 13. Each daily event has three short questions: a bond to ten, a bridging question and one subtraction back to ten.

The answers sit below a gap, and the bridging question includes a tip in plain words for parents.

How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/make-ten-math-calendar-for-kids/make-ten-daily.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

What age is this for?

Mostly 5–8, around kindergarten to Grade 2, but older kids who struggle with carrying benefit too.

How long does it take?

One or two minutes. Keep it light and stop while it's still fun.

What's Number Beasts?

A turn-based number-bond puzzle game about making ten and carrying the rest.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
