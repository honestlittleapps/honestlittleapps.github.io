How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/brain-puzzle-of-the-day-calendar/brain-puzzle-daily.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

How hard are they?

Mostly medium. Classic trick questions, sequences and short logic problems, suitable for teens and adults.

Can I use them in class or at work?

Yes. They make good warm-ups for meetings and lessons.

Is there a game version?

Cognilo is a daily set of five short brain puzzles for adults, across memory, logic and attention.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
