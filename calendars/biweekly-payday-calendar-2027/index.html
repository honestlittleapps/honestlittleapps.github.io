How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/biweekly-payday-calendar-2027/biweekly-payday-2027-a.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Which schedule is mine?

Find the date of your first paycheck in January 2027. Jan 8 or 22 means A. Jan 1 or 15 means B.

Are there 27 paydays in 2027?

Not on these two Friday schedules. Each has 26 paydays in 2027.

What does EarnCheck do?

EarnCheck records your shifts, estimates gross pay and compares hours with your pay statement. No bank or employer login needed.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
