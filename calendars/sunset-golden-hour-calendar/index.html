How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/sunset-golden-hour-calendar/sunset-new-york.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

How accurate are the times?

They're calculated with the Astral astronomy library for each city centre at sea level, typically within a minute of official tables. Local terrain can make the sun disappear earlier.

What's golden hour?

The period before sunset when the sun is low and the light turns warm and soft. Here it's defined as the sun being between 6° above and 4° below the horizon.

My city isn't listed.

Asterfold works anywhere and shows where the sun will set from your exact viewpoint.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
