How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/weekly-focus-check-calendar/weekly-focus-check.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Which test should I use?

Any consistent one works. Attention Age gives a five-minute set of attention challenges and keeps your weekly results.

How long until I see a trend?

Four weekly checks is the minimum before reading anything into it.

Is this a medical test?

No. It's for personal tracking. Talk to a clinician about persistent attention problems.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
