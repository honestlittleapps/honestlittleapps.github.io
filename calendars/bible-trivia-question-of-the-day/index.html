A trivia question takes thirty seconds, and that's exactly why it gets asked. Each day of the month has one question about the people, places and events of the Bible, with the answer and the reference below it so you can look it up together.

The questions range from easy (Who killed Goliath?) to ones that catch adults out (What was Paul's name before he was called Paul?). Use it at family devotions, with a youth group or as a warm-up for small group.

How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/bible-trivia-question-of-the-day/bible-trivia-daily.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Is it suitable for kids?

Yes. The questions are family-friendly, and most are answerable by kids who know the main Bible stories.

Where are the answers?

In the same event, below a short gap, so you can guess before you see them.

Is there more?

Scrollway is a free Bible quiz game with ten mini games and short lessons. It works offline, needs no account and has no ads.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
