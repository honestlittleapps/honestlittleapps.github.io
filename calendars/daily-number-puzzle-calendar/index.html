How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/daily-number-puzzle-calendar/number-puzzle-daily.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Is there always an answer?

Yes. Every puzzle was solved by computer before it went in the calendar.

Can kids play?

Confident 9–12 year-olds usually can. Younger kids can try using all five numbers with plus only.

What's Sumbriska?

A calm number puzzle game where you flip bridges until each island reaches its target sum.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
