Galatians 5:22–23 is easy to memorize and hard to live. This calendar slows it down: one fruit gets a whole week, and then the cycle repeats. It runs about five or six times a year, so each fruit comes round again before you've forgotten it.

Every week starts with the Greek word behind the fruit, a key verse and three practical steps. Choose one each day. Families and small groups can share one subscription and talk about it on Sunday.

How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/fruit-of-the-spirit-weekly/fruit-of-the-spirit.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Can a small group use this together?

Yes. Everyone subscribes to the same link, so the whole group is on the same fruit each week.

What's the Greek word for?

It's a small extra for anyone curious. Each fruit's original word has a meaning that's easy to look up, like makrothumia ('long-tempered') for patience.

How is Ninefruit different?

Ninefruit is a free app that turns Galatians 5:22–23 into small daily steps you can track. The calendar is a simple weekly reminder.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
