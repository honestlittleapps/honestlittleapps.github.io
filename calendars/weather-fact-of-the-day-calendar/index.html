How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/weather-fact-of-the-day-calendar/weather-fact-daily.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Where are the facts from?

NOAA, the US National Weather Service and the World Meteorological Organization's records. Figures are rounded to be easy to remember.

Is this a forecast?

No. For forecasts and warnings, use your national weather service.

What's RushOrb?

A one-thumb 3D adventure where you switch the weather to clear the path.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
