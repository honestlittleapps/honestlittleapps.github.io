Self-employed tradespeople and service pros usually pay federal income tax in quarterly estimates. This calendar lists the 2027 due dates, adjusted where the usual date falls on a weekend or holiday, plus the February 1, 2027 deadline for sending 1099-NEC forms for 2026.

It also builds two money habits: chasing overdue invoices on the 1st of each month, and sending every estimate from the week before Friday afternoon. Tax dates can change, so always confirm on irs.gov. This isn't tax advice.

Questions

Why is Q4 on January 18, 2028?

January 15, 2028 is a Saturday and the next Monday is a federal holiday, so the deadline moves to Tuesday. The IRS confirms this each year, so check irs.gov.

Does it cover state taxes?

No. Many states have their own estimated tax dates.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
