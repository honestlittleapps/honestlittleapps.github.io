How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/subscription-audit-calendar/subscription-audit.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Where do I see subscriptions on iPhone?

Settings › tap your name › Subscriptions. That covers App Store subscriptions. Others (streaming, gyms) show up on your card statement.

Does this connect to my bank?

No. It's just a reminder calendar, and nothing is linked.

What does MoneyShield do?

MoneyShield tracks subscriptions and free trials with cancel-by reminders, without connecting to your bank.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
