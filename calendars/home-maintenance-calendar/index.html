The home maintenance calendar: three jobs a month

Gutters in April, dryer vent in May, hoses before the first frost. Three small jobs land on the first Saturday of each month, in the right season, so nothing big sneaks up on you.

Home maintenance: December checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Check holiday light cords for damage. • Know where the main water shut-off is and label it. • Clear snow and ice paths; stock ice melt.

Jan2Sat
Home maintenance: January checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Replace or check the HVAC filter. • Test the sump pump if you have one. • Check for drafts around doors and windows.

Feb6Sat
Home maintenance: February checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Clean the range hood filter. • Check grout and caulk around tubs and showers. • Vacuum fridge coils.

Mar6Sat
Home maintenance: March checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Check the roof and gutters after winter storms. • Book an A/C service before the spring rush. • Flip or rotate mattresses.

Apr3Sat
Home maintenance: April checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Clean gutters and downspouts. • Check outdoor faucets and hoses for freeze damage. • Inspect deck and steps for loose boards.

May1Sat
Home maintenance: May checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Clean window screens and check for tears. • Test GFCI outlets (press TEST, then RESET). • Clear dryer vent lint from the outside duct.

Jun5Sat
Home maintenance: June checklist

This month's three jobs (about an hour in total): • Check grab bars and stair rails are solid. • Trim plants back from the house and A/C unit. • Clean the dishwasher filter.

Most expensive home repairs start as small jobs nobody remembered: a clogged gutter, a lint-packed dryer vent, a hose left connected through a freeze. This calendar spreads the year's maintenance into three jobs a month, timed to the seasons.

Each month also includes at least one safety item, like grab bars, stair rails, night lights or non-slip mats. That makes it useful if you help keep a parent's home safe as well as your own.

How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/home-maintenance-calendar/home-maintenance.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Why the first Saturday?

It's the easiest slot for most households. Move it to whenever suits you.

Is this for renters too?

Many items are. Skip the structural ones and report problems to your landlord.

What does RoomMend do?

RoomMend is a room-by-room home checklist and fix planner with large buttons, for your home or a parent's.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
