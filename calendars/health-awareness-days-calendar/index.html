Health observances are useful hooks for lessons, newsletters, workplace wellness and social posts, but they're scattered across WHO, UN and charity websites. This calendar collects 18 fixed-date global days and repeats them every year.

Each event names the organiser and gives one plain fact about the body or the condition. Days with moving dates (like World Kidney Day, the second Thursday of March) aren't included, so the dates here are always right.

How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/health-awareness-days-calendar/health-awareness-days.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

Who sets these days?

Most are WHO-recognised or UN observances. Others are run by bodies like the World Heart Federation or the Union for International Cancer Control. Each event names its organiser.

Is it good for classrooms?

Yes. Teachers use awareness days as lesson starters, and each fact here is short enough for a slide.

What's Body Unpacked?

An interactive health education app that follows a food or nutrient into the body and explains the evidence behind it.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
