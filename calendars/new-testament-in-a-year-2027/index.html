A year-long plan only works if you can recover from a missed day. Keeping weekends free does that. Each weekday shows one chapter, in order from Matthew 1 to Revelation 22. On the first day of each book you also get a short introduction: who wrote it, who it was for, and what to watch for.

Every event also carries one question to think about. There are five that rotate, like "What did the first readers most need to hear here?", so a three-minute reading turns into a little study.

How to add it on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Add to iPhone Calendar, then tap Subscribe.
  3. Tap Add. Events appear in the Calendar app within a minute.

On a Mac, the same button opens Calendar. In Google Calendar, use the Google button, or paste https://honestlittleapps.github.io/calendars/new-testament-in-a-year-2027/new-testament-2027.ics into Other calendars › From URL.

Questions

I'm starting late. Can I still use it?

Yes. Subscribe any time and pick up with that day's chapter, or use weekends to catch up on the chapters you missed.

Why weekdays only?

260 chapters fit the 261 weekdays of 2027 almost exactly. With weekends free, one missed day doesn't put you behind for good.

How does Wayfold help?

Wayfold is a chapter-by-chapter Bible study app. It gives questions, shows the verse behind each answer, and has printable small-group leader guides.

Is this calendar free?

Yes. It costs nothing, needs no account and shows no ads. You can unsubscribe at any time.

How do I remove it from my iPhone?

Open the Calendar app, tap Calendars at the bottom, tap the ⓘ next to this calendar and choose Delete Calendar. Or go to Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
