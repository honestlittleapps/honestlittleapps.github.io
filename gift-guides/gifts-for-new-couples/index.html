Gift Ideas for a New Relationship

A few dates in and a holiday is coming. You want it to say “I've been paying attention” without saying “I've planned our wedding.” These hit that balance.

Small and thoughtfulExperience giftsWhat to skip early onFree gestures

No sponsored picks and no affiliate links. These are ideas, not products, so you can find them wherever you like to shop. Pair any gift with one of our free printable gift tags.

Small and thoughtful

Their favourite snack or drink, from something they mentionedShows you listen.
A book you think they'd like, with a short note insidePersonal without being heavy.
A plant for their placeEasy to give and easy to keep.
A candle or cozy socksSafe, warm and low-pressure.
A mixtape-style playlistFree, personal and fun to make.

Experience gifts

Tickets to something they mentionedA show, a game or a local event.
A class to take togetherCooking, pottery or climbing.
A planned day outYou do the planning; they just show up.
Ice skating or a holiday lights walkSeasonal and fun.
A picnic or homemade dinnerTime is the real gift.

What to skip early on

Expensive jewelleryIt can feel like pressure in the first months.
Gifts that need a long commitmentLike a pet or a year-long subscription.
Joke gifts about their looksEasy to misread early on.

Free gestures

A handwritten cardSay what you've enjoyed about getting to know them.
Plan the next date and tell themSomething to look forward to.
Remember a detail and follow upAsk how the thing they were nervous about went.

Questions

How much should you spend on a gift for someone you just started dating?

Most people keep it modest early on. Something small and thoughtful matters more than the price.

Is it weird to give a gift after a few dates?

A small, thoughtful gift around a holiday is usually welcome. Keep it light and personal.
