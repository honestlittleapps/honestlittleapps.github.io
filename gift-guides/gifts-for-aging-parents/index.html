Gifts for Aging Parents

The best gifts for older parents make life a little safer and easier without making them feel watched. Here's a list that does both.

Safety without fuss

Motion-sensor night lightsLight the way to the bathroom at night.
A medical alert buttonChoose one together, so it feels like their decision.
Grab bars or a shower seat, professionally installedBathrooms are a common place for falls.
A battery-powered lantern and radio for outagesSimple to use when the power goes out.
A large-button phone or a simplified phone setupMake calling family effortless.

Questions

What do you buy an elderly parent who has everything?

Experiences, help and connection: regular visits, rides, meal deliveries, a photo book or help organising important information.

How do I give a safety gift without offending them?

Frame it as your peace of mind, involve them in choosing, and pair it with something fun.
