Gift Ideas for Christian Men

Most men say “I don't need anything.” These ideas are practical, personal and built around what he actually values.

Grow his faithPractical everyday giftsExperiences and time

No sponsored picks and no affiliate links. These are ideas, not products, so you can find them wherever you like to shop. Pair any gift with one of our free printable gift tags.

Grow his faith

A journaling BibleWide margins for notes make reading more active, and it becomes a record of his walk.
A men's devotional for the yearOne short reading a day is easier to keep than a big plan.
A leather-bound prayer journalSomewhere to write down prayers and look back at how they were answered.
A study guide for one book of the BibleA focused guide (Proverbs, James or Mark) is less daunting than the whole Bible.
A framed verse that means something to himPick the verse he quotes most, or the one from his wedding or baptism.

Practical everyday gifts

A quality pocket knife or multitoolUsed daily, lasts for years, and can be engraved.
A good thermos or travel mugFor the early commute or the job site.
Work gloves or boots he'd never buy himselfPractical upgrades are often the most appreciated.
A sturdy flashlight or headlampFor the garage, the car and power cuts.
A simple watchA quiet reminder to be on time and off his phone.
A cast-iron panIf he cooks, it's a gift he can pass on.

Experiences and time

A breakfast with you, once a monthPut the dates in the calendar as part of the gift.
A men's retreat or conference registrationTime away to reset is often the gift he needs most.
A day fishing, hunting or hiking togetherTime outdoors with no agenda.
A handwritten letterTell him specifically what you respect about him. Men rarely hear it.
A day of projects done for himWash the truck, fix the fence, clear the garage.
Tickets to a game or concertAn evening together with something to look forward to.

Questions

What is a good gift for a Christian man?

Something that fits his daily life and his faith: a journaling Bible, a devotional, a quality tool he'll use every day, or time together doing something he loves.

What do you give a pastor for Christmas?

Many pastors value a handwritten note of thanks, a gift card for a meal out with their family, or a day off covered by volunteers.
