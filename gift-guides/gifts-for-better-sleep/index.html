Bedroom upgrades

Blackout curtains or a good sleep maskDarkness tells the body it's night.
A quality pillowAn old, flat pillow is a common, cheap fix.
Breathable cotton or linen sheetsMany people sleep better when they don't overheat.
A white-noise machine or fanSteady sound masks sudden noises.
A warm, dimmable bedside lampSoft light in the evening instead of bright overhead light.

Questions

What is a good gift for someone who can't sleep?

Practical bedroom upgrades like blackout curtains, a sleep mask, a white-noise machine or a better pillow are popular. If poor sleep lasts for weeks, encourage them to talk to a doctor.

Do sunrise alarm clocks work?

Many people find waking to gradually brightening light gentler than a loud alarm. Results vary from person to person.
