Questions

How much should I spend on a teacher gift?

There's no expected amount. Many families spend under $25, and a heartfelt note from the child is often the most valued gift. Some schools have rules on gift values, so check first.

What do teachers want most?

Teachers often say handwritten notes, gift cards they can use, and classroom supplies top their list.
