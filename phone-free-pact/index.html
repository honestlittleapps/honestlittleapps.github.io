Why a pact beats a resolution

A resolution is a promise to yourself, and those are easy to renegotiate at 11 pm. A pact is shared: someone else knows the rules, and keeping them protects time you spend together. Start small with one or two rules. Add more once they stick.

Questions

What is a phone-free pact?
A short, shared agreement about phone use, like no phones at dinner or phones charging outside the bedroom, made and signed by two or more people.
What are good phone rules for couples?
Start with one or two: no phones at the dinner table, no scrolling while you're talking, and phones charging outside the bedroom.
How does the co-sign link work?
Your pact is packed into the link itself. Your partner opens it, reads the rules and taps Co-sign to finish the card. Nothing is stored on a server.
Can families use it?
Yes. Put 'the Lee family' (or any name) as the second signer, and print the card for the fridge.
