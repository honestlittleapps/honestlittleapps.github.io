Questions

What is a blessing letter?

A letter from a parent that names what they love and see in their child and prays God's blessing over them. Many families give one at birthdays, baptisms, graduations or before a child leaves home.

What should I include?

Specific things you love about who they are, gifts you see in them, a memory, your prayers for them and a Bible verse.

Is my letter saved anywhere?

No. It stays in your browser until you print it.
