Questions

What do you write in a letter to your future self?

Describe your life right now, your worries and hopes, what you're proud of, and questions for your future self. Small details (what you ate, a song you love) are often the best part to read later.

How do I make sure I open it on time?

Use the calendar reminder button. It adds an event on the date you choose, with an alert that morning.

Is my letter uploaded or saved?

No. Your letter stays in your browser until you print it.
