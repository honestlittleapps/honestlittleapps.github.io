How to make them

  1. Choose 6 to 24 moments and print the labels.
  2. Write each letter by hand using the prompt.
  3. Seal each one and stick on its label.
  4. Tie them together with ribbon, or keep them in a box.

Questions

What are open when letters?

A set of sealed letters, each labelled for a moment like “open when you miss me”. Your partner opens the right one when that moment comes.

How many open when letters should I write?

Anywhere from 6 to 24. Even a small set of six well-written letters makes a meaningful gift.

Are these good for long distance?

Yes. Open when letters are especially popular for long-distance couples and deployments.
