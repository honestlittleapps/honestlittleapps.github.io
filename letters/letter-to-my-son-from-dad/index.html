Questions

What should a father write to his son?

Be specific about what you're proud of in his character, share a lesson you learned the hard way, say what you hope for him, and end with simple advice he can keep.

When should I give it?

Birthdays, graduation, a wedding, leaving home or any ordinary Tuesday. Some dads write one each year and keep them for later.

Is my letter saved anywhere?

No. It stays in your browser until you print it.
