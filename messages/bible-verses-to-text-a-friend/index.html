Questions

What is a good Bible verse to text a friend who is struggling?

Psalm 34:18 (“The LORD is nigh unto them that are of a broken heart”) and Isaiah 41:10 (“Fear thou not; for I am with thee”) are two that people often find comforting.

Which Bible translation is used?

Every verse is from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.

Should I add my own words?

Yes. A short personal line like “Thinking of you today” makes a verse feel like it came from you, not from a list.
