Questions

What do you write in a teacher's Christmas card?

Thank them for something specific (patience, a subject your child now loves, how they handled a hard day) and wish them a restful break. Two or three sentences is plenty.

Is a note enough without a gift?

Many teachers say a heartfelt note is the gift they keep longest. A handwritten line from the child is especially valued.

What about teacher's aides and specialists?

There's a section above for aides, coaches, librarians and bus drivers, who often get missed.
