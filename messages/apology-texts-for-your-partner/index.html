Apology Texts for Your Partner

A real apology names what happened, owns it and offers repair. These texts do that in a few honest lines. Add their name, copy it, and send it.

What makes an apology land: say what you did (not “if you were upset”), say how it affected them, and say what you'll do differently. Skip the “but”.

Questions

How do you write a sincere apology text?

Name what you did, acknowledge how it affected them, and say what you'll do differently. Keep it short, and avoid “I'm sorry if you felt…” or adding a “but”.

Should I apologise by text or in person?

A text is a good first step, especially if they need space. Bigger hurts usually also need a face-to-face conversation.

What if they don't reply?

Give them time. One sincere message is better than several in a row.
