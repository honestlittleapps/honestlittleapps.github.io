Questions

How often should I check in on an elderly parent?

It depends on their health and independence. Many families agree on a simple daily text, plus a call or visit each week. Agree on it together so it feels like connection, not supervision.

How do I bring up safety without upsetting them?

Ask rather than tell, explain it's for your own peace of mind, and offer to do it together. The texts in “Gently raising safety topics” are written that way.

What if they don't reply to a check-in?

Agree in advance on what happens if they don't answer by a certain time, such as a call, then a neighbour checking. Agreeing early avoids panic later.
