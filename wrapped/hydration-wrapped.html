About this card

The card multiplies your daily average by 365 and converts it to liters and US gallons. A bathtub is counted as roughly 150 liters, a typical comfortable fill, so the comparison is a picture rather than a precise measure.

Questions

How much water should I drink a day?

Needs vary with body size, activity and climate. The US National Academies suggest about 3.7 liters of total water a day for men and 2.7 liters for women, including water from food. Your doctor can advise you personally.
