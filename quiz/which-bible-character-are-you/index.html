Questions

Is this quiz for youth groups?

Yes. It works well as a youth group or small group icebreaker. Have everyone take it, then read each character's story together.

Which Bible translation is used?

Verses are from the King James Version.

Where can I read more about my character?

David: 1 Samuel 16 to 1 Kings 2. Ruth: the Book of Ruth. Peter: the Gospels and Acts. Esther: the Book of Esther. Moses: Exodus to Deuteronomy. Joseph: Genesis 37 to 50.

Is my answer saved or sent anywhere?

No. The quiz runs in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored.
