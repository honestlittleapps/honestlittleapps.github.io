Questions

Is this an addiction test?

No. It's a quick, light look at the pattern behind your phone use, so you can pick a change that fits.

What's the easiest first change?

For most people it's switching off notifications that don't come from a real person. It takes two minutes in Settings.

Is my answer saved or sent anywhere?

No. The quiz runs in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored.
