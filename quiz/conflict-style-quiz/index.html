Questions

Where do these five styles come from?

They're loosely based on a well-known model of five conflict modes (competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding and accommodating), written in everyday language for couples.

Is one conflict style the best?

No. Each has strengths. Couples tend to do best when each person can step outside their default when the moment needs it.

Should we both take it?

Yes. Send the link to your partner, then compare. Knowing each other's default makes the next disagreement easier.

Is my answer saved or sent anywhere?

No. The quiz runs in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored.
