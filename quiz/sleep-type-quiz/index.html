Questions

Is this a medical sleep test?

No. It's a light quiz about your habits and preferences. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, snoring or daytime sleepiness, talk to a doctor.

Can my sleep type change?

Yes. Age, light exposure, work and routines all shift when you naturally feel sleepy and alert.

Is my answer saved or sent anywhere?

No. The quiz runs in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored.
