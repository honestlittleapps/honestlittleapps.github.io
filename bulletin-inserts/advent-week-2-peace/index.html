The text (copy it into your newsletter, slides or email)

“For unto us a child is born, unto us a son is given: and the government shall be upon his shoulder: and his name shall be called Wonderful, Counsellor, The mighty God, The everlasting Father, The Prince of Peace.” — Isaiah 9:6 (KJV)

The busiest month of the year is the one named for the Prince of Peace. Peace here is not a quiet calendar; it is Someone carrying the weight we can't. This week, hand one worry back to him.

This week at home: Monday: Isaiah 11:1–10 · Tuesday: Luke 1:67–79 · Wednesday: Philippians 4:4–9 · Thursday: John 14:25–27 · Friday: Psalm 85

Talk about it: What is one thing you could stop carrying this week?
