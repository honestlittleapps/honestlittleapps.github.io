Tricks for the 6s

  • 6 × a number = 5 × the number + one more of the number: 6 × 7 = 35 + 7 = 42.
  • When you multiply 6 by an even number, the answer ends in that number: 6 × 4 = 24, 6 × 8 = 48.
  • 6 × a number is double 3 × that number.
