Tricks for the 11s

  • For 11 × 1 to 11 × 9, repeat the digit: 11 × 4 = 44.
  • 11 × 10 = 110, 11 × 11 = 121, 11 × 12 = 132.
  • 11 × a number = 10 × the number + the number once more.
