Tricks for the 9s

  • Finger trick: hold up 10 fingers and fold down finger number n. Fingers on the left are tens, on the right are ones.
  • For 9 × 1 to 9 × 10, the digits of the answer add up to 9.
  • The tens digit is one less than the number you multiply by: 9 × 7 = 63.
