Tricks for the 7s

  • 7 × a number = 5 × the number + 2 × the number: 7 × 6 = 30 + 12 = 42.
  • Remember 7 × 8 with 5, 6, 7, 8: 56 = 7 × 8.
  • Most of the 7s already appear in other tables, so only 7 × 7 = 49 is truly new.
