Tricks for the 3s

  • Skip count by 3s: 3, 6, 9, 12, 15…
  • The digits of every answer add up to 3, 6 or 9 (for example 27 → 2 + 7 = 9).
  • 3 × a number = 2 × the number + the number once more.
