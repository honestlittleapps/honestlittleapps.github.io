The Make-Ten Strategy

The mental-math trick that turns 8 + 5 into 10 + 3.

to 5to 10to 20to 100Make ten

The make-ten strategy turns hard additions like 8 + 5 into easy ones. Take part of the second number to fill up the first number to 10, then add what is left.

Step by step: 8 + 5

  1. 8 needs 2 more to make 10 (a bond to 10).
  2. Split 5 into 2 and 3.
  3. 8 + 2 = 10, then 10 + 3 = 13.
