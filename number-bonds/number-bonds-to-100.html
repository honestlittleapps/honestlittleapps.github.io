Tips

  • Start with tens: 30 + 70 = 100 because 3 + 7 = 10.
  • For numbers ending in 5, the partner ends in 5: 35 + 65.
  • For any number, make the next ten first, then the jump to 100: 38 → 40 (2), 40 → 100 (60), so 38 + 62 = 100.
