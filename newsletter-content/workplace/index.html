Better-sleep tip
Lights down, screens off or dimmed, and one slow minute of breathing (in for 4, out for 6). A consistent wind-down cue helps your brain recognise bedtime. Aim for the same bedtime and wake time, even on weekends. That's one of the best-supported sleep habits.

Shared from Sleepward: Bedtime Routine, a free iPhone app.

Money minute: the subscription audit
Monthly check: • iPhone: Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Cancel anything you didn't use last month. • Scan your card statement for small recurring charges. • Note any free trials and their cancel-by dates. A $9.99 charge you've forgotten is about $120 a year.
