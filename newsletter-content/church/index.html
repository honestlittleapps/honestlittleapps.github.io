December 2026

This month's fruit of the Spirit
The fruit: Patience (Greek: makrothumia). Galatians 5:22–23 lists nine fruits of the Spirit. This month, focus on one. Key verse: James 1:19. Pick one step a day: • Let the other person finish talking every time today. • Pick the longest checkout line on purpose. • Wait 24 hours before replying to something that annoyed you. At the end of the month, ask yourself where you saw this fruit grow, and where it didn't.
