Brain teaser of the month
If 5 machines make 5 widgets in 5 minutes, how long do 100 machines take to make 100 widgets? Give it 60 seconds before you look. Answer: 5 minutes. Each machine makes one widget in 5 minutes.

Make 10: a number-bond challenge
Three quick ones: 1) 9 + ? = 10 2) 7 + 5 = 10 + ? 3) 18 − ? = 10 Tip for 2: give 3 from the 5 to the 7 to make 10, and see what's left. Answers: 1) 1 2) 2 (so 7 + 5 = 12) 3) 8
